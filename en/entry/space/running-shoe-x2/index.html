What's the value?

The value of this article is 0.58 m, obtained from the base reading of "The outer length of the sneakers" 0.29 m times 2. The base entry measure is the Euro42 maximum outer length of adult sneakers along the soles of the shoe; conversion does not change the physical object, it changes only on which scale we put it. [1]
