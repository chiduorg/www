Burj Khalifa

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The construction in Khalifa was 828 metres high. The high-rise database records this measurement condition separately from the height at the top of the maximum device, so it is not possible to mix the different "ceiling" definitions into the same group.

What is it?

Al-Khalifa is a top-up building in Dubai, located in the centre of the city, with a retreating beam structure. The station compares “building heights” from the lowest visible open-air entrance to the top of the building, which contain tipping points but does not include functional equipment such as antennas, flag poles, etc. [1][2] It was completed in 2010 with 163 floors above the ground and is a hybrid verticality. [1]

How?

This altitude is not visualized by photographs, but by detailed drawings submitted by the owner, which are verified and confirmed by the High Building and City Habitat Council. [2] Validation is required to confirm the position of the entrance level, whether the tower is a building block and whether roofing equipment should be excluded. The Sclap Centre registers three heights at the same time: construction height, top-of-the-art equipment, top-use floors, with different readings. [1] Therefore, a first reading of the column used in the registration form is required.

Value and Measurement condition

The height of the building is 828 metres, the tipping point is 829.8 metres and the maximum floor is 584.5 metres. [1][2] Three figures answer different questions and this article uses only 828 m. If antennas or functional devices are included, they should be replaced by 829.8 metres of measurement convention; if a person is asked how high it can reach, see 584.5 metres. It can also post variations such as roof heights, mast heights, but none of these are part of the same "building heights". [1]

Common misunderstandings

“828 metres from the ground to the tip of the antenna” is not accuracy: the height of the building excludes functional equipment such as the antenna, while the tower tops belong to the building. Nor is 828 metres considered habitable, with a maximum of 584.5 metres on the floor and a total difference of more than 200 metres. [1][2] Another misunderstanding is to treat height as a mass or volume indicator; the altitude only describes vertical distance and does not represent material usage or floor size. For the same building, the three numbers would not be combined into one number by asking for a comparison.

Relationship with other scale

828 metres is about 7.9 times the 105-metre edge of the football field and about 9.4 per cent of Everest 8848.86 metres high. It is about 0.024 per cent of Moon ' s diameter compared to 3474.8 kilometres; it is smaller than the Earth ' s diameter of 12742 kilometres. It remains the local height of the city and does not determine the baseary of the city. [1]
