What is it?

Galaxy is a gravitational restraint system consisting of dozens to thousands of galaxy and is also filled with high-temperature gases and large amounts of dark matter. This page takes the typical horizontal diameter of the “Fully Galaxy Regiment” to about 10 million light-years, approximately 9.46×10²² metres, as an overview of this type of object scale. [1][2]

How?

The Galaxy regiment was first identified through a grouping of red migrations and projection distributions, and then judged by the speed at which members were dispersed to determine whether they were bound by gravity. X-ray observation can see tens of millions of degrees of gas between galaxy, and weak gravity lenses can name total mass, including dark matter. [1] According to Chandra, the galaxy group ' s diameter is usually millions of light-year, while a consolidated galaxy group system can span about 10 million light-year. [1]
