Laptop Computer

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The standard model of MacBook Air 13 inch (M5) is 13.6 inches and about 345 mm; the serial name "13 in" is not the same as the measured diagonal line.

What is it?

This article uses Apple MacBook Air 13 inches (M5) as the standard model to compare the diagonal lines of the screen. The type is equipped with a 13.6-inch diagonal screen, approximately 345.4 mm. [1] "13 inch" is the product series name, which actually shows a diagonal line of 13.6 inches; it cannot be replaced by a series of names. The “13 inches” seen at the time of purchase are serial names and the specification page gives a true diagonal line, which cannot be used in combination.

How?

Apple Technical Specification Page indicates in the display column "13.6 inches (aragonal) LED backlight screen" and lists resolution and pixel density. The Mac page of Apple also labels the machine MacBook Air 13 inches (M5), corresponding to the 13.6 inches of the specification page showing the diagonal line. [2] Screen diagonal lines are measured by visual display area and do not contain frames, cavities or casings.

Value and Measurement condition

13.6 Inches equals 345.44 mm, usually written about 345 mm or 34.5 cm. [1] This number describes the diagonal line of the luminous area of the screen, not the aeroplane width, the depth of the aeroplane or the diagonal of the packaging. If the border is factored in, the whole diagonal line will be larger; if the 16:10 scale is shown, the width and height are approximately 293 mm and 183 mm, respectively, the extrapolation value, not the plant trademark.

Common misunderstandings

The screen of the "13-inch notebook is 13-inch" does not show an exact diagonal line of 13.6 inches: MacBook Air 13-inch. It is also not considered to be the length of the airframe; it also has keyboard surfaces, interfaces and rotors, with a three-dimensional dimension. Another misconception is to mix the 13-inch type of machine from different years. M1, M2, M3, M4 and M5 may have different display specifications and must lock the model. [1][2]

Relationship with other scale

345 mm is about 1.16 times the length of 297 mm for A4 paper and 0.86 times the width of 40 cm for adult shoulders. It is approximately 36 mm shorter than the 15-inch machine of approximately 381 mm diagonal and suitable for a single shoulder bag to carry. [1] Compared to the height of the mobile phone, 147.6 mm, the diagonal line on the laptop screen is approximately 2.3 times its height.

This article uses SI metre-based length units, following the BIPM definition of the metre. [3]
