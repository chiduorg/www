Human Hand

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The hand is the scratching organ at the end of the front limb. A human measurement study published in the Bulgarian medical journal measured an average of approximately 178.62 mm (women) and 187.39 mm (men) for the full length of adults; 20 cm was used as a reference for the full length of adults.

What is it?

The hand consists of a scratching organ at the end of the front limb and consists of a wrist joint, a wristbone, a palm bone and a fingerbone; the thumb has two fingers and the remaining four fingers have three. The thumbs can be turned against the other fingers, so that humans can complete the exact and powerful grip. [2] This article compares the full length of the hand, from the number of wrist crosses to the length of the middle finger, not the length of the hand, palm or single finger.

How?

A study by PubMed included 500 tested persons aged 15-78, 250 men and women, and measured hand sizes by six age groups. Its summary gives an average of approximately 178.62 mm for women and 187.39 mm for men and an average of approximately 73.45 mm for women and 87.66 mm for men. [1] Britannica ' s hand intestry indicates that the hand consists of a wristbone, a palmbone and a fingerbone, and that the hand-to-hand relationship between the thumb and other fingers is an important feature of human hand function. [2]

Value and Measurement condition

20 cm equals 200 mm and 0.2 m. It is a comparison of the full-hand length of adults, which is closer to the upper end of the above-mentioned study and is higher than the female average. [1] The position of the wrist of the tested person at the time of the test, whether or not the finger is stretched straight, whether it is a wrist cross or a wrist arthropod sign changes the outcome; therefore, the “hand length” is not a fixed value that can be used outside the measurement protocol. Wide hand and long hand are two different axes and cannot be replaced by one another.

Common misunderstandings

The common misconception is to consider hand lengths as “long hands” or “broad hands”. The total length of the hand consists of the finger and the palm of the hand is only one part of it; the hand is wide along the other side towards measurement. [1][2] Another misunderstanding is that the average value of a given study is used as a criterion for all: gender, age, region and occupational groups affect hand size, and 20 centimetres is the single value selected for daily comparison in this article and does not represent every adult.

Relationship with other scale

Twenty centimetres of hand are slightly longer than 17.78 centimetres of pencils, nearly nine times larger than 22.25 mm coins, and about 2,000 times wider than 100 micrometre hair. [1] It is also the most easily accompanied “foot” in the scale of the human body: the length of the article is roughly the length of the wrist to the middle. Up is the height, arm and body, down is the pencil, coin, grain of rice and cell.

This article uses SI metre-based length units, following the BIPM definition of the metre. [3]
