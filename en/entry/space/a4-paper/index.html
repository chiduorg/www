A4 Paper

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The long edge of A4 paper is 297 mm, which is defined as a cropping dimension according to ISO 216; this article does not mix long edges with short edges, diagonal lines or whole-port heights.

What is it?

A4 Paper is one of the sizes of the series A specified by ISO 216 and is often used for office printing, writing and tables. This compares the long edge of the paper, i.e. 297 mm, instead of the short edge 210 mm, the diagonal line or the stack height of the whole paper. [1] The series A is based on the length ratio of 1:2 and A0 is folded to A1, then to A4.

How?

The value is derived from the size table of the standard text instead of taking a ruler to measure a piece of paper. ISO 216: 2007 Table 1 lists A0 841 x 1189 mm, A1 594 x 841 mm, A2 420 x 594 mm, A3 297 x 420 mm, A4 210 x 297 mm. [2] ISO standard pages specify the size of paper written and certain printed materials and apply to paper for administrative, commercial and technical purposes. [1] Therefore, 297 mm is a standard size; the actual product allows for a tailor-made difference, but does not change the nominal value.

Value and Measurement condition

Long edge 297 mm, short edge 210 mm, long width ratio of 2:1 and approximately 0.06237 m2. [2] These are nominal dimensions. If 29.7 cm, 0.297 m or 11.69 inches are reported, it is only in different units; if 297 mm "paper thickness" or "package height" is reported, it is another amount. Paper gw, thickness and plane size are described by different criteria.

Common misunderstandings

Do not mix the long edge of A4 with the diagonal line: the diagonal line is approximately 363.7 mm, clearly greater than 297 mm. Nor is it to equate A4 with American Letter paper; Letter is 216 x 279 mm, with shorter, wider, shorter edges. [2] Another misunderstanding is that all A4 paper is strictly 297.0 mm; the standard is given to a nominal tailor size, and the difference between production and tailoring is to be treated according to the corresponding product specifications.

Relationship with other scale

297 mm is about 1.6 times the length of the adult palm 18-19 cm and about 3.5 times longer than a bank card 85.60 mm. Compare the long edge of A4 with the short edge of A3, which is 297 mm, which is the relationship. [2] On the desk it is a single page scale, three to four orders of magnitude. [1]

This article uses SI metre-based length units, following the BIPM definition of the metre. [3]
