What is it?

The Centauri constellation is the largest spherical constellation of galaxies with about 100 thousand Stars and a total of about 4 million solar mass. [1] It has a near-spherical shape, is highly dense, is old and has low metal abundance. It may not be an ordinary spherical constellation, but rather a remnant of small galaxy that has been annexed by the galaxy.

How?

The diameter of the spherical constellation is determined by light measurements and Star count: the outer star face is bright from the core until it reaches the outer margin beyond the background. [1][2] ESA ' s description gives about 150 light-years diameter and about 15,800 light-years distance. [1] Britannica points out that the spherical constellation is a symmetric cluster of older Stars, with more than 150 galaxies. [2] Star counts are subject to the deduction of foreground and background stars and the separation of member stars by colour-stars.
