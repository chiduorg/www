Strong Force Range

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The strong interaction is linked to the kinetics of quarks and glues, as well as toom nuclear levels showing residual inter-nuclear effects. Before discussing its “scope”, it was necessary to describe the subject of the study, the energy conditions and the model, and not to find a single hard-cut-off radius.

What is it?

Strong interaction is one of the basic interactions described in standard Model and is derived from signal "colour", transmitted by glue. [1][3] At the quarker level, it binds quarker into proton and neutron; at the nuclear level, its residual effects combine the proton and neutron into atom. [1][2] Both levels are referred to as “strong”, but the subject, the effective description and the physical quantity of the study are not the same and cannot be described only as a nuclear mass.

How?

The accelerator experiments test quantum colour dynamics by deep, non-flexical dissipation, injections and the generation of strong, with grid-point calculations giving the strong structure from the theoretical side. [3] Britannica summarises the short range of the force to about 10⁻¹⁵, close to the diameter of Proton or Neutron, while DOE states that it is about 100 times more powerful than electromagnetic and has approximately a distance of about 10⁵ than the atom diameter. [1][2] These are the descriptions of order of magnitude, not the hard cut-off from a particular instrument.

[1][3] Toom nuclear radius is used directly as a powerful force range and also as a mixture.

Common misunderstandings

Painting power into the same spring that connects all the nuclear weapons can mislead people to believe that force suddenly falls to zero after crossing a certain distance. In fact, when quarke's distance increases, it increases until energy is enough to generate a new quarke-back quarke; at a shorter distance, freedom is closer. [3] “Strength 100” is also a comparison in the vicinity of protonscale and cannot be extended to a fixed multiplier at any distance. [2]

Relationship with other scale

On the scale axis, the nuclear charge radius is about 0.84 femtometres, carbon-12 nuclear 2.47 femtometres, uranium-238 nuclear 5.86 femtometres, and atom as a whole about 0.1 nanometres. [4] The range of powerful order of magnitude is similar to that of the atom nuclear size, but the "activity range" "nuclear radius" and "nuclear diameter" are still different; first, the length of the comparison should be clarified.
