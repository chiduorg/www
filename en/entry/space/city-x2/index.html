City × 2

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

2.000×10^05m, based on1.000×10^05m of "city ", obtained 2.000×10^05 m by ×2 conversion; used to connect the adjacent scale anchor, not the new independent measration.

What's the value?

The value of this article is2.000×10^05m, obtained from the base reading of "city" 1.000×10^05 times 2. The base intestry measure is the maximum range of East and West of Shanghai City; conversion does not change the physical object, it changes only on which scale we place it. [1]

How's that?

The calculation is subject to gradual review:1.000×10^05m x 2 =2.000×10^05m. If the base value is replaced by another unit and the same multiplier is applied, the result must return to the same order of mass; the unit, the multiplier and the signatureficant figures must be recorded together, and not leave only one number. [2]

Why keep this anchor?

This anchor is used to connect "city" to neighbouring intestry: when the reader asks "What does it feel like to be bigger/smaller?", the article can be directly defined without having to be recalculated. It is suitable for the contrast position of the scale steps, combinations or object pages. [3]

With the neighbor scale.

When the article is lined up with Khalifa (828 m); the Golden Gate Bridge (2737 m), see first whether the unit is unified and then whether the reference is in diameter, radius or or orbital distance; the same number may be twice or even more different under the same series. The baseline provides a readable starting point: city does not have a natural unified diameter. This article, which is based on a sample of the Shanghai City District of China, uses a maximum span of about 100 km from east to west as a comparable value to reflect the differences between administrative areas, population concentration areas and the contact areas of Commuting. [1][3]

How does Error pass it?

Error follows the baseline entry: the base value significant figures determine the results of the conversion; the multiplier is a precise integer or simple fraction and does not add to the measurementor. [1][2] If the baseline value itself is a approximation, the limitation of “approximate” should be retained thereafter.

♪ Bang, bang, bang ♪

This article is not a new observation or a substitute for baseline; it does not change ID, slug or canonical, but only adds a recalculated scale reference. Citiation should be accompanied by a reference intestry and multiplier to avoid misreading the conversion value as an independent measurement. [3]

The common misconception is to treat the results of the conversion as an independent measurement of “new discoveries”. It is actually an arithmetical projection of the base value; without a new observation, sample or model, error would not have been reduced by taking a pretty multiple. This article shall be accompanied by a reference, factor and unit. [2] [3]

Final check: the baseline contains several credible numbers, and the results of the review keep several, without copying the end of the calculator. [1][2]
