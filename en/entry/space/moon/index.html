Moon is not a completely geometrical sphere. Why do you always turn to the same side?

Moon didn't turn around. Its rotation is roughly synchronized with that of the Earth, so from the Earth's perspective, it is mainly towards us in the same hemisphere. Moon's back will also be lighted by the sun, and the “backside” will not be the same as the eternal dark side. The day-to-day moon vision comes from a combination of the sun and the direction we observe and cannot be interpreted as the shadow of the Earth blocking Moon. [2]

The error book of this value.

Moon averages about 3,474.8 kilometres in diameter, about 0.273 times the Earth. This value is derived from the combination of Moon laser ranging, occult and orbiter mapping; Moon is not a perfect sphere and has a slightly flat and volatile shape. When writing 3,475 km in diameter, error is at the kilometre scale from the average radius definition and terrain benchmark, rather than “Moon is shrinking”.
