Dining Chair Height

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The overall height of the dining chair is 0.90 metres, from the ground to the top of the back of the chair; the surface height is an additional 0.45 metres, and there are no substitutes for the two scenarios.

What is it?

There are two commonly used meals chairs: the overall height, from the ground to the top of the back, about 0.90 m; the surface height, from the ground to the surface, about 0.45 m. The main value of this article is the overall height and the surface height is further noted. The difference between the two is about 0.45 meters, and it's about the part above the seat by the back.

How?

The manufacturer ' s specifications mark the overall height of the PINNTORP dining chair at 0.90 metres and the surface height at 0.45 metres, as a specific example of a common market-based approach; different back styles, cushion thickness and seat leg height change readings. [1] Human performance information emphasizes the need for seat heights to flatten both feet and approximate levels of thighs, which is why 0.45 metres are commonly used. [2] Table heights and surface heights need to be considered in pairs; changing chairs without changing tables narrows the kneespace.

Value and Measurement condition

0.90 meters is the overall height, 0.45 meters is the surface height and the two cannot be replaced. The seating position is high and the overall height determines the support range of the back to the back and the space required for receipt. The measurement shall be carried out on the hard ground, and the cushion will sink when the human body sits down and the nominal value is usually given in an unpressured state. [1] If the radians of the seat belt, the highest point may not be in the middle line, the maximum value in the vertical direction shall be taken. If on a soft carpet, the chair will sink and the reading may be a few millimeters smaller than the hard ground.

Common misunderstandings

Treating 0.45 metres as "the height of the chair" underestimates the overall size; treating 0.90 metres as the height of the surface results in chairs that are not available. [1][2] The dining chair is not the same as the bar chair, the office chair, which usually covers 0.60-0.75 metres, while the office chair emphasizes the adjustable range and does not directly fit the table. The chair with the arm also needs to be at an additional level, which does not fit into the overall height.

Relationship with other scale

0.90 meters are about 44% of the gate height of 2.032 meters and about half the height of 1.75 meters. [1] The surface is 0.45 metres close to the common range of adult calves up to the height of two laptops. The overall height of the dining chair is about half the size of the refrigerator, 1.8 metres, and there is sufficient surplus to move into a normal door hole.

This article uses SI metre-based length units, following the BIPM definition of the metre. [3]
