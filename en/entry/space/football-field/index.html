Football Field

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The IFAB rules set the length of the edge of the international competition site at 100-110 metres and the length of the goal line at 64-75 metres. This page selects 105 metres as a reference for the length of the football field and does not describe a particular display as a uniform requirement for all games.

What is it?

The football field is rectangle. The amount of this article is the length of the 11-person court, i.e. the length of the line, not the opposite line, the goal line or the outer edge of the watch. The site is also marked with the middle line, mid circle, penalty area, corner arc, etc., which change the layout of the site and do not change the length of the edges. The goal is itself of a different size and cannot be mixed with the space length.

How?

The International Football Council Rules of Competition provide that the length of the international competition is 100 - 110 metres and the length of the goal is 64 - 75 metres; the FIFA guidelines recommend 105 metres by 68 metres. [1][2] Rules are given scope and guidance is given to commonly recommended values at different levels. The rules also provide that the width of the line does not exceed 12 cm, with a radius of 9.15 m in the middle circle. Measurement should be taken along both ends of the border, without an off-site buffer zone and billboards. The rules also require that the goal line be vertically and flatly, and that the internal side of the line or the centre of the line be harmonized in the sample description.

Value and Measurement condition

A reference of 105 metres is recommended and not necessarily the same for every field. The length of 105 m is the same, and the width of 68 m is another; the length of the door is 7.32 m and the height of the door is recorded separately. [1][2] Approximately 7140 m2 of site area is obtained using 105 m by 68 m, but the area cannot reverse the exact value of a particular edge. The size of the youth, the five-person system or the training field varies.

Common misunderstandings

“Foot stadiums are exactly 105 meters” is not in place: international competitions allow 100 to 110 meters, and grass-roots venues can be different. Nor can the 100 yards of the American football field, about 91.4 metres, be the same object. [1][2] Another misunderstanding is to consider the length of the border as size or to replace the long edge with a diagonal line, which changes with width, while 105 m describes the long edge. When comparing different fields, long, wide and measurebound should be given simultaneously.

Relationship with other scale

The 105 m is about one eighth of 828 m in Khalifa, about 26 times longer than the total length of the Golden Gate Bridge. Compared to Everest, which is 8848.86 metres high, it represents about 1.2 per cent of the latter. Put it in city scale, it's just block length, not city diameter. [1][2]

This article uses SI metre-based length units, following the BIPM definition of the metre. [3]
