What is it?

The main asteroid belt is an area of long-term operation between Mars and Jupiter in the solar system and a large number of asteroids. It is not a dense solid ring, but an orbital belt of countless objects that are very distant from each other. Common misunderstandings

The common misconception is that the asteroid belt is as crowded as in the film and that spacecraft must constantly elude; in fact, the average distance between asteroids can be millions of kilometres, and only a small part of the total mass inside is Moon mass. Another misconception is that all asteroids are in the main belt; the Trojans, NEOs and the Koiber belts are not. The master belt has also been described as a “failed planet”, and even more so as Jupiter's gravity has prevented the material from uniting into a planet. [2]
