Basketball

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Basketball is a round balloon. The diameter of this article is derived from the perimeters specified in the rules of the game: 749-780 mm, corresponding to approximately 23.8-24.8 cm in diameter and 24 cm in length, of the 7th ball of the man.

What is it?

Basketball is a round-shaped balloon with a suture or gutter on the surface, which is easy to hold. The diameter referred to in this article is the largest straight distance from which the sphere passes through the heart of the ball; the rules of the game usually provide for the perimeter, so that the diameter here is obtained by the perimeter conversion rather than by measuring the face directly with a calculus. [1][2]

How?

The basketball competition rules published by the Nanjing University of Industry School of Physical Education state that the ball may not be less than 0.749 metres in round weeks or greater than 0.780 metres. [1] The FIBA 2017 Basketball Equipment Rules published by the Japan Basketball Association listed basketball as a rule-bound game equipment, stating that its specifications were not arbitrary. [2] The rule gives a perimeter range requiring a diameter to be conversioned at a time.

Value and Measurement condition

The perimeter 749-780 mm divided by a circular rate is approximately 238-248 mm in diameter, i.e. 23.8-24.8 cm; the article takes 24 cm as the diameter reference for the man 7 basketball. [1] This value is not the direct result of the decision, but the full value from the rule perimeter. The sphere is not perfect, and the inflatability, the position of the seams and the position of the measurement all cause a millimetre-grade difference. The female 6 ball and the teenage ball are smaller and cannot share 24 centimetres with the ball 7. [2]

Common misunderstandings

The common misconception is to use the "24 cm diameter of basketball" as the original rule. The rules actually provide a perimeter of 749-780 mm and a diameter of only conversion values. [1] Another misconception is to ignore the number of balls: men 7, women 6 and young people with different sizes, and to describe all basketballs in the same diameter as if they were not true. Others mix diameter with perimeter, resulting in more than three times the size difference.

Relationship with other scale

Twenty-four centimetres of basketball is about three and a half times the diameter of tennis, slightly larger than football, and much larger than ping-pong, about 1.5 times the length of a human hand. [1][2] On the scale axis, the ball is concentrated in the daily man-made belt “a few centimetres to twenty centimetres”: books, bats and bodies up, coins, pencils and tablets down. Compare basketball, football and tennis with the same “diameter” to avoid the mixing of perimeter and diameter.

This article uses SI metre-based length units, following the BIPM definition of the metre. [3]
