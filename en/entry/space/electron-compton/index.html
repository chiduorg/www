What is it?

How?

Compton observed in experiments where X-rays were dispersed by electron that the wavelength of the post-dispersion photons was longer and that the variation was related to the dispersive angle; this could not be explained by classical fluctuations, but was consistent with the energy and momentum of the photons-electron collision.
