The vertical dimensions of the length of the domestic car between the outermost end of the safety bar are taken from 4.5 metres as a common reference for compact to medium sedans, with the actual pattern being 4.4-4.8 metres.

What is it?

The length of the household car refers to the vertical size of the front-to-back-to-back-to-out end of the front, excluding rear-vision mirrors, antennas and trailers. This article takes 4.5 metres as a common reference for compact to medium sedans, with the actual pattern being between 4.4 and 4.8 metres. [1]

Car sizes are determined by the plant ' s trademark and included in the product specifications, and the Measurement benchmark is usually an empty, standard tyre-pressed profile.
