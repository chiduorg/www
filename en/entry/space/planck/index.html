Planck Length

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Plank length is about 1.616 × 10⁻³⁵ m given by approximate Planck constants, 10,000 gravitational constants and light speed combinations. It's theoretical scale, not space pixels that's observation.

What's Plank length?

Planck length is a combination of approximately Planck constants, gravitational constants G and light speed c, as LP=√ (ħG/c3). It places the effect of quantum mechanics, the strength of gravity and the speed of relativity in the same expression, and is therefore often seen as scale in the discussion of quantum gravity. [1][2] It is not the size of an object, nor is it a ruler that has been calibration to 10⁻³⁵ M; it is more like a coordinates inside the theory, which are used to mark the time of classical space descriptions that may begin to fail. [1][3]

Theorydefinition and observation

This article must be divided into two layers: the formula and the values are derived from the theory and constant measurement, and Planck's length itself has never been directly measured. NIST gives values calculated by a combination of constants, not the length read by an instrument. [1] Telescopes and accelerator detection structures are much larger than it; Britannica also states that it is much smaller than the ground accelerator can discern. [2] The specific scene of quantum gravity is still model and Plank length does not automatically prove that time and space consists of discrete units. [3]

Value and Measurement condition

2022 CODATA gives 1.616 255×10⁻³⁵ metres, standard uncertainty 0.000 ZXXPH1QX meters, relative to uncertainty approximately 1.1×10⁻⁵, mainly from gravitational constant G. [1] If you change to the Planck constant h instead of the platinum, the expression and the value will be different; the profile will have to state which agreement is used, and two definitions cannot be mixed. [1][2]

Common misunderstandings

The common misconception is that Planck length is the smallest pixel of space. Existing experiments do not show space dissegregating on this point; it simply states that the quantum effect of gravity cannot be ignored when the known theory describes a shorter distance. [1][3] Another misunderstanding is to treat it as a particle diameter; Proton is twice as large as it is about 10²⁰, and any statement that “particles are as big as Planck's length” confuses feature scale with entity baseary. [2]

Relationship with other scale

On the scale axis, it is located below the preton (approximately 1 fly-me, 10⁻¹⁵ metres) of about 20 order of magnitude and is much smaller than atom (approximately 10⁻¹⁰ metres). [2] Next to them are Planktime and Planck temperature, which are also a combination of constants that together constitute a theoretical reference rather than a sequence of objects that have been taken. [3] Read it separately from “the smallest scale of theoretical discussion” and “the smallest structure of experimental determination”.

Why haven't you seen it yet?

Planck's length is about 20 order of magnitude; to distinguish it directly, it requires about 10¹⁹ GeV order of magnitude, far above the current collision. Existing experiments can only cap “no new structure on this scale” and cannot prove that space is separated from Plank length. [2] This is also the reason why the quantum gravity theory lacks direct testing: it is not the object that a telescope can see, but rather the characteristic scale within the theory. [1][3]

Distinction from Quantum Foam

Quantum foam is an image of a potentially dramatic rise and fall in Plankcale time and space, a theoretical assumption, not a filmed foam structure. [3] The length of Planck is only a ruler for the length of this discussion; it does not prove that time and space consist of foam, nor is it equal to the size of a particular rise or fall. Using both terms as synonyms, the term “theoretically” is misinterpreted as “observation objects”. [1][3]

Five-axis Contrast

On the space axis, it is 10⁻³⁵ m; on the time axis, it corresponds to 5.4×10⁻⁴⁴ seconds; on the temperature axis, it corresponds to 1.4×10³² K; on the mass axis, it corresponds to 2.2×10⁻⁸ kg. Each of the four volumes is a combination of the same constants, conversion, but not equivalent to the four entities already detected. [1][3]
