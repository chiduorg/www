Dog

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Dogs are domestic animals in carnivores. The length of the stem of this article is from the shoulder joint to the skeletal section, with no tails; reference is made to the 12-month average of Kars shepherds of 72 cm.

What is it?

The dog (Canis lupus familiriaris) is a domestic animal in carnivores, which is domesticated by a grey wolf. The length of the body referred to in this article is the surface distance of the body from the shoulder joint to the skeletal section, excluding the tail, the front head and the back leg. [1][2]

How?

One of the PMC studies provided a measurement of the surface size of Kars sheepdogs at the age of 12 months, with an average length of 72.04 centimetres and shoulder height of 69.34 centimetres. [1] Animal Diversity Web records the body mass range of domestic dogs from less than 1 kg to 70 kg, indicating significant body size differences among different species. [2] One gives an average body length for a given species, and one explains the overall difference between the dogs, together with a reminder that a number cannot be used to represent all the dogs.

Value and Measurement condition

This article takes 72 centimetres as a long reference for Kars sheepdogs of 12 months of age, an average of 72.04 centimetres from the study. [1] It is not the mean length of all dogs, nor does it represent larvae, geriatric dogs or other species. Measurement usually allows the dog to stand naturally, measuring the length of the shoulder joint to the skeletal knot, without including the tail; position, hair and individual differences influence readings. The weight range of ADW ranged from less than 1 kg to 70 kg, which is an indication of the same size. [2]

Common misunderstandings

The common misconception is that 72 centimetres are considered "standard size of dogs." In fact, small dogs may be only 20 or 30 centimetres and large dogs may exceed one metre; the diversity of varieties is much greater than that of measurementor. [1][2] Another misunderstanding is to mix the long body with the high shoulder and the long tail: the high shoulder is the vertical distance from the top of the ground to the shoulder, and the long tail also includes the tail, which is not the same size.

Relationship with other scale

72 cm is approximately three to four times the length of a human hand, longer than a cat, shorter than an adult, and much smaller than elephants and giraffes. [1][2] On the scale axis, dogs span a range of “tens of centimetres to one metre” and are the most dispersed category of domestic animals. The size of the body is defined by “species plus age” in order to allow it to be compared fairly with the size of cats, rats, humans and others.

This article uses SI metre-based length units, following the BIPM definition of the metre. [3]
