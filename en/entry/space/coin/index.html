Coin

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The diameter of the fifth version of the 2019 edition was adjusted from 25 mm to 22.25 mm. This page is based on this specification published by the People ' s Bank of China, which compares the diameter of the coin with not the thickness, weight or old version of the coin.

What is it?

One coin is one of the coins in circulation. The fifth version of the 2019 version, with the adaptation of specifications while maintaining the material, still has the face of the nominal number “1”, but the diameter has been reduced, adding to the contour the security features of invisible drawings and additional dots on the edges. [1] [2] This article compares the outer circle diameter of the coin, not its thickness, weight or purchasing power.

How?

The People ' s Bank of China issued a communiqué in April 2019 and issued a fifth set of 2019 RMB on 30 August. The interpretation reproduced by the Chinese Government Network clearly states that the diameter of a coin is adjusted from 25 mm to 22.25 mm, and the material remains unchanged. [1] The People ' s Bank, reproduced by the Press Office of the State Council, further explained that the adjusted specifications had been reduced by 11 per cent in diameter and were easily accessible to the public. [2] Two official sources agree on 22.25 mm.

Value and Measurement condition

22.25 mm equals 0.02225 m and 2.225 cm. It is the largest diameter of the outer circle of a coin, usually within the currency plane; the thickness, the angle of the edge, and the height of the sculptor do not belong to that number. [1][2] Old version 1 coins 25 mm in diameter, reduced to 22.25 mm in 2019, cannot be used in combination. The official version of the “minus 11 per cent” is a change in the relative old diameter, not an absolute error.

Common misunderstandings

The common misconception is that “all one dollar coins are 25 mm”. The 2019 version has changed the diameter to 22.25 mm, and the old version may feel close to the new version in hand but with different specifications. [1][2] Another misconception is that the diameter is considered to be thickness or weight: the thickness and mass of the coins are specified separately and cannot be generated by 22.25 mm. When comparing coins, the figures are meaningful only if they are “the version, the face, the diameter or the thickness”.

Relationship with other scale

22.25 mm coins are larger than 5 mm grain of rice, much wider than 100 micrometres of hair and less than 20 centimetres of human hands. [1] It is appropriate as a reference for “cm-grade daily objects”: four coins with a row of approximately 8.9 cm, close to a part of the width of the hand. Upward are hands, faces and bodies, down are grains of rice, sand, dust mites and cells.

This article uses SI metre-based length units, following the BIPM definition of the metre. [3]
