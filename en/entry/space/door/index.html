Standard Door Height

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The nominal height of the door inside the house is often 80 inches, or 2.032 m; it is the height of the door, not the level of the net opening of the door, nor the width of the door.

What is it?

The standard door points high to the labeled height of the door fan in the house. The common inner door in North America is 80 inches, or 2.032 metres, or approximately 6 feet 8 inches. [1] This number describes the doorpiece itself, without door frames, thresholds and floor decorations; the net height of the door hole is reduced by the position of the door frame and the hardware.

How?

Minnesota rules in the section “Established building evacuation corridors” state that the door may not be less than 80 inches (2,032 mm). [1] British Columbia, Canada, has built codes that require a net opening to evacuate a doorhole at a height not less than 2,030 mm. [2] Two figures are close, but one lecture is high, one is clean and the subject is not exactly the same. While the former gave a threshold for the height of the door, the latter stated a net opening height, the normative provisions were different and readings were not directly interchangeable.

Value and Measurement condition

80 inches multiplied by 25.4 mm equal to 2,032 mm, which is 2.032 m used in this article. [1] 2,030 mm is the norm lower limit for net openings, which are usually slightly higher than net openings, taking into account the door frames, the thickness of the doors and the installation of a public difference. [2] If the door fan is 2.032 m high, the doorhole structure is also fitted with frame material, which is common at about 2.1 m. In Chinese homes, 2.0 m or 2.1 m and in Europe 2.0 m class are common, indicating that 2.032 m is only the common measurement condition in North America.

Common misunderstandings

The "door high is the door high" is not accuracy: there are three things about the height of the door, the height outside the door frame and the height of the net opening. [1][2] Nor can the 2.032 metre be treated as a uniform standard for all countries, with common highs in Europe, Japan and China. Another misconception is to mix the door wide; there is another set of accessibility and evacuation requirements for width. There is a further detail: a door-opener can remove a single amount and a door-hole can be measured with the door frame.

Relationship with other scale

2.032 meters is about 1.16 times higher than the size of a person, 1.75 meters, more than double the size of a dining chair of 0.90 meters. [1] It is equal to about 1.13 times the length of a bicycle of 1.8 metres and is placed in a driveway of 3.5 metres wide, with a horizontal line of 1.7 doors. The gate is only about 7% tall compared to 28,65 meters in the basketball court.
