Betelgeuse Diameter

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The IV diameter depends on an angle diameter, distance and surface definition. The study cited in 2022 uses approximately 1,000 solar radiuss as a working assumption, according to which this page gives a comparative diameter of about 1.39×10¹² metres.

What is it?

Bed IV is Orion's red superstar and mass is about 10 to 20 times the sun. Its “diameter” is not a hard side of a direct measration, but a scale produced by angular diameter, distance and surface definition. [1] Red superstars are expanding late in evolution, with lightballs low and large radiuss.

How?

Star Interference and spectraobservation give an angular diameter and multiply it with a distance to obtain a linear radius; distance itself relies on vision and model. [1] Dupree et al. used R* = 1000 R☉ as a working assumption for the calculation of the extended atmosphere in 2022 when analysing the `big dimification' of BQ4 and noted studies from Ohnaka et al. [1] The article does not treat 1,000 R☉ as a new direct measurement. Interference gives different angle diameters in different bands because the star layers correspond to different bands.

Value and Measurement condition

Based on 1,000 solar radiuss and an average solar volume equivalent radius of 695,700 km, the radius is approximately 6.957×10¹¹ m and the diameter of 1.3914×10¹² m. [2] This is a model value and is not the only dimension of permanent change in board; the combination of distance and angular diameter gives different results. [1] This number corresponds to the "Twice radius under the 1,000 R☉ scenario". If the smaller radius is changed, the diameter is reduced accordingly; the article does not seek a single “real value”.

Common misunderstandings

It is a common misconception to mix the diameter of Zhuqing with its distance to Earth, or to think that the red superstar must be bigger than all Stars. The diameter is object scale, distance is another matter. [1] Another misunderstanding is that the outer edge of the luminous or extended atmosphere is factored into the light ball diameter; observation defination usually refers to the light ball layer. The reporting radius or diameter must be explained in conjunction with the distance and surface definition.

Relationship with other scale

1.39×10¹² is approximately 9.3 au, about 1,000 times the diameter of the Sun. [2] Put it in the solar system, which can extend in diameter to near Jupiter ' s orbit; however, this size is still very small compared to the distance it travels to Earth. 9.3 Au is also approximately 9.3 times the solar-terrestrial distance and can accommodate multiple inner planetary orbits.
