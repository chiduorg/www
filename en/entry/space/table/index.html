Dining table length

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Reference is made to the long side of the table when the table is unexpandable, approximately 180 cm; the measure is long, not high or wide.

What is it?

This is the long edge of the table table. Take a specification that stretches the table and represents it in an open state: the desktop is 180 centimetres long, 90 centimetres wide, can sit for six people, and can be increased to 240 centimetres at the extended edge. [1] There are no tables high or space occupied by chairs. The table is one of the most common horizontal scale in the family, and the long side decides how many chairs can be rowed together.

How?

The manufacturer ' s specification page removes the size of the same table into four dimensions: a minimum length of 180 cm, a maximum length of 240 cm, a width of 90 cm and an altitude of 75 cm. [1] Another table in the same series and a package of six chairs also lists “the minimum length of the table 180 cm and the maximum length of the table 240 cm”. [2] Both locations are based on the outer edge of the desktop and do not account for scalable panels into undisturbed status.

Value and Measurement condition

180 cm equals 1.8 m and 1800 mm. It describes the outer limit of the long edge of the desktop when it is not expanded; it increases by 60 centimetres to 240 centimetres after expansion. [1] The actual land will be larger if the legs of the table are tilted or included under the table. The table is 75 centimetres high, which is the size of the other direction and cannot be combined with the long edge. [1]

Common misunderstandings

The first misperception is to treat the “six table” as a fixed size: the table may be 160, 180 or 200 cm, with the article taking only 180 cm. The second misunderstanding is to consider the table high as long: 75 centimetres of table height close to the adult hip, more than double the long edge of the table of 180 centimetres. [1] There are also those who consider the expanded 240 cm as the default length; the comparison should indicate whether it is not or is not. [1][2]

Relationship with other scale

1.8 Mets are approximately 19 centimetres long for adults 9.5 times the length and about 6 times the length of 29.7 centimetres for A4 paper. It's about 2 meters taller than the usual door, and it's about 1.8 meters wide of a car. [1] Compared to the diagonal line of the laptop screen of 34.5 centimetres, the table is approximately 5.2 times its length; when sitting in three rows, each person can be assigned to a table line of about 60 centimetres. After expansion, 240 centimetres also change the distance from the table to the wall; if the chair is placed at an open 180 centimetre, it is temporarily moved when it is spread.

This article uses SI metre-based length units, following the BIPM definition of the metre. [3]
