Hot spring water temperature × 0.5

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

On the basis of the "spa" 313 K, 156.5 K by 0.5 conversion; used to connect the adjacent scale anchor, not the new independent measurement.

What's the value?

The value of this Article is 156.5 K, obtained by the base reading of the "Spa" 313 K multiplied by 0.5. The baseline is the water temperature of the spring or bath; conversion does not change the physical object, it changes the level on which scale we put it. [1]

How's that?

The calculation is subject to gradual review: 313 K x 0.5 = 156.5 K. If the base value is replaced by another unit and the same multiplier is applied, the result must return to the same order of mass; the unit, the multiplier and the signatureficant figures must be recorded together, and not leave only one number. [2]

Why keep this anchor?

The anchor is used to connect the hot spring to the adjacent intestry: when the reader asks “what it feels like to be bigger/smaller”, it is possible to provide this article directly without having to be recalculated. It is suitable for the contrast position of the scale steps, combinations or object pages. [3]

With the neighbor scale.

When rowing this article alongside sauna air (353 K); deep-sea hydrothermal (623 K), see first whether the unit is uniform and then whether the reference is in diameter, radius or or orbital distance; the same number may be twice or more different under the different measurement condition. The baseline entry sumary provides a readable starting point: the hot springs for human impregnation are more than 30-45 °C, which takes 40 °C (313 K). It refers to water temperature in springs or baths, not thermal reservoir; differences between vents, ponds and post-temperature measurements must be explained. [1][3]

How does Error pass it?

Error follows the baseline entry: the base value significant figures determine the results of the conversion; the multiplier is a precise integer or simple fraction and does not add to the measurementor. [1][2] If the baseline value itself is a approximation, the limitation of “approximate” should be retained thereafter.

♪ Bang, bang, bang ♪

This article is not a new observation or a substitute for baseline; it does not change ID, slug or canonical, but only adds a recalculated scale reference. Citiation should be accompanied by a reference intestry and multiplier to avoid misreading the conversion value as an independent measurement. [3]

The common misconception is to treat the results of the conversion as an independent measurement of “new discoveries”. It is actually an arithmetical projection of the base value; without a new observation, sample or model, error would not have been reduced by taking a pretty multiple. This article shall be accompanied by a reference, factor and unit. [2] [3]

Final check: the baseline contains several credible numbers, and the results of the review keep several, without copying the end of the calculator. [1][2]
