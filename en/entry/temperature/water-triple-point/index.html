Water triple-point temperature reference

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The three water phase points are assigned a value of 273.16 K, or 0.01 °C, corresponding to pressure 611.657 Pa. It is a state of reference where ice, liquid water and water vapour coexist; since 2019, Kelvin has changed from Bolzmann constant to definition.

What is it?

The three points of water are ice, liquid water and water vapour coexisting and reaching the state of the motherl equilibrium, with an ITS-90 grant value of 273.16 K, or 0.01 °C, corresponding to pressure 611.657 Pa. [1][3] It is not an "0 °C ice point" but a specific balance point of 0.01 K above the normal pressure point. The three-point water must also be made up of isotope from the Vienna Standard Average Seawater (VSMOW), otherwise the ratio of hydrogen and oxygeniisotope will change temperature. [1]

How?

The three phase points are carried out in sealed three phase containers: the packaging contains water consisting of a particular iiiisotopepeope, vacuuming to about 611.657, Pa, and forming several mm thick ice caps around the central hotbed. The freezer was initially adapted and needed to recant the fire in the water bath for at least 10 days; before measurement was used to briefly insert a temperature measuring well with a room-temperature glass bar to form a thin layer of melted water that would allow the ice cap to move freely. [1] The standard platinum resistance temperature is then applied to the temperature-testing well and is then read after the motherl equilibrium. The actual three phase of the laboratory-level container is distributed within approximately 50 μK. [1]

Value and Measurement condition

273.16 K - 273.15 K = 0.01 K, or 0.01 °C; 611.65 Pa = 6.11657 hPa, or 0.6 per cent of Earth's sea level gas pressure. [1] The point of ice under constant pressure is 273.15 K, 0.01 K below the three-phase point, as air solubility and pressure move balance. 273.16 K of ITS-90 is the value of the thermostat; after 2019 Kelvin is definitation by the Boltzmann constant, the mothermodynamic temperature of the real water three-point phase needs to be accompanied by uncertainty, not “absolute accuracy on the definition”. [2] [3]

Common misunderstandings

The water three-point 0 °C ignores the differences and pressure conditions of 0.01 K: the constant-pressure point is 273.15 K, the three-point phase is 273.16 K. [1][3] The second misunderstanding is that there is no need for a specific pressure at the three points; 611.657, Pa is a necessary condition for the existence of the three points, and when the pressure deviates, it is no longer a triangulation. Thirdly, it is considered that 273.16 K remains definitionkelvin; the 2019 revision has been changed to the Boltzmann constant. [2] Finally, the ice water mixture in the ordinary refrigerator is not a three-phase container and cannot be used as a calibrationpreciation device.

Relations with other temperate areas

273.16 K is 0.01 K higher than the normal pressure ice point 273.15 K, 100 K below the boiling point of water 373.15 K and about 31 K below the carbon dioxide threshold 304.18 K. [1][3] It is well above absolute zero and below ethanol boiling point 351.5 K. The three water phase points are therefore located in a narrow window “near the ice point” and are a key anchor point for connecting cryogenic physics and thermostats on the temperature chain.
