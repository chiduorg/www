Corning 7740 glass working point

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Corning's 7740 boron silicate glass table lists the work points corresponding to viscerality 10⁴poise as 1252°C, approximately 1525 K. This article uses clear card numbers and sticky indicators to avoid the use of glass processing temperaturee as a common melting point for all glass.

What is it?

It's not about some kind of "consolidated melting point for glass," it's about Coring 7740 boron silicate glass. The glass is cooled without a fixed melting or condensing temperaturee, as is the case with crystals, but with a gradual hardening of the viscous liquid; the project uses a viscous temperaturee to describe the processing phase. [2] 7740 is sodium boron silicate glass, often used in heat-resistant vessels and laboratory glass.

How?

The table of material properties for Corning gives a feature point by viscerality: the work point corresponds to 10⁴poise, i.e.10³ Pa.s, temperaturee is 1,252 °C; the same table also lists the soft spot 820 °C (10⁷6 poise), the retreat point 560 °C (10¹³poise) and the response point 510 °C (ZXXPH4QZ Zpoise). [1] These values are derived from the standard stickyness of the manufacturer ' s plate No. 7740, and are not a case changeobservation for a piece of glass, nor is it given to the item.

Value and Measurement condition

1,252 °C conversion is 1,525.15 K, displaying about 1,525 K. [1] The work point describes the viscosity area where glass can be formed, pulled or blown, and does not amount to “smelted furnace temperaturee” or to the start of softening. Soft points, retreat points and response points are corresponding to higher viscosity, lower temperaturees, respectively; treating 1,525 K as a melting point for all glass would confuse the material with the material. [1][2] The glass of different formulations, water content and thermal history, with the same viscosity, varies.

Common misunderstandings

First, glass is considered to have a fixed melting point like ice; glass is a lack of long-range, orderly solids and transformation is a continuous kinetic process. [2] Second, use the work point as “the temperaturee of the furnace that must be heated”; the actual temperaturee of the furnace is usually higher than the work point and depends on the formation process. Third, the value Corning 7740 is applied to sodium calcium glass, stone glass or fibreglass; they have different viscosity curves. Fourth, ignore the sticky unit: 10⁴poise equals10³ Pa.s, not10⁴ Pa.s.
