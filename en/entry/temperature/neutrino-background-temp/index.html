What is it?

The neutrino background in unverse is the distribution of the relics left behind by the early mass decoupling of universe. It is similar to the universe microwave background, but carries neutrinos rather than photons. Standard thermal history suggests that early universe neutrons, electrons, positive Electrons and photons shared the same temperature; as the universe swelled, the weak interaction rate fell rapidly, the neutrons retreated around 1 MeV.
