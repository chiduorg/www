What's the value?

The value of this article is 2,803.32 K, obtained by base reading of "Molniya" 28,033.2 K multiplied by 0.1. The baseline is high temperature of lightning-heated air; conversion does not change the physical object, it changes the level on which we put it. [1]

How's that?

The calculation is subject to gradual review: 28,033.2 K x 0.1 = 2,803.32 K. If the base value is replaced by another unit and the same multiplier is applied, the result must return to the same order of mass; the unit, the multiplier and the signatureficant figures must be recorded together, and not leave only one number. With the neighbor scale.

Lines this article with the solar surface (5772 K); the Earth's thermal gas temperature (1000 K) see first whether the unit is uniform and then whether the comparator is in diameter, radius or orbital distance; the same number may be twice or more different under the different measurement condition. The baseline entry summary provides a readable starting point: lightning will rapidly warm the air near its corridors. The United States National Meteorological Service describes this heating with the use of the order of magnitude, which can reach about 50,000 °F, which amounts to approximately 28 million K; this refers to the heated air and the channel plasma, which cannot treat the charge movement itself as a fixed temperature object. [1][3]

Thermospheric gas kinetic-temperature reference

The aerodynamics of thin thermosphere gases are usually around 500-2,000 K, where 1,000 K is taken as an example. It describes the mean kinetic energy of the gas particles, which is not the equivalent of the temperature that must have been reached by the object at that altitude.
