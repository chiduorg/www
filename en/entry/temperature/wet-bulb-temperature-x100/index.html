What's the value?

The value of this article is 30,800 K, which is obtained by the base reading of the "temperature 35°C: physical upper limit of thermal stress" of 308 K multiplied by 100. Base entry is 308 K (35°C wet ball temperature); conversion doesn't change the physical object, it changes the level on which we put it. [1]

How's that?

The calculation is subject to gradual review: 308 K x 100 = 30,800 K.
