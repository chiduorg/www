Temperature / Room temperature

Room-temperature example

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Select here 20°C, or 293.15 K, as a daily example of the indoor environment. Room temperature varies according to location, time period and use situation, and this convenient reference point cannot be described as the best temperature or life activity available to all rooms.

First pick a reference around you.

This station uses 20 °C to represent an example of an indoor environment corresponding to 293.15 K. This value is chosen by the project and is intended to allow the reader to move from the familiar temperate zone; it is not a measration result for all rooms. NIST's temperature science programme also uses about 21 °C as a daily example, so a convenient integer should not be read as the only global “room temperature definition”. [1][2]

How do you match kelvin?

The two thermometers have different zeros, but the same unit spacing. On the basis of definition, the value of temperature equals the value of Kelvin temperature minus 273.15; 20 °C and 293.15 K describe the same temperature. If the temperature difference is 5 °C, or 5 K, from 20 °C to 25 °C, the temperature difference is calculated without adding 273.15. [2]

20 °C is not 10 °C "twice heat"

Zero points of the °C are subject to fixed deviations, so 20 is divided directly by 10 and the physical temperature is not available. Even if kelvin were to be compared, it would be a comparison of the corresponding thermodynamic temperature, which does not mean that one object must contain more energy than the other. The unit K of temperature is different from the unit J of energy; the other conditions of object and substance cannot be omitted. [1][2]

What else do you need for room temperature?

If the term “room temperature” is written in the laboratory or material description, an additional condition should be given for actual temperature and materialization, which cannot replace readings. The 20 °C in this paper does not simultaneously specify the location of humidity, pressure, sample composition or measurement, nor is it the best condition for all life activities, material preservation or reaction processes. Readers can use it as a hot zone sign, and specific conditions are still required for specific issues.

Keep moving from the hot zone.

The 20 °C can be compared to the ice point of water, the temperature of human body or the boiling point of water, first looking at what is measured by each entry and then looking at the temperature. This station is organized according to KelvinReference values, and toggle a degree Celsius displays only a change of reading expression, which does not mean that all objects in the image are going through the same heating process. Packagings, colours and particles are used for speaking purposes and are not real-time indoor temperature records.

The othermodynamicsteperature unit of this article is based on BIPM's definition for Kelvin. [3]
