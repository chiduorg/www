Temperature / Mid temperature

Bread oven setpoint reference

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Bread baking has no uniform material. This is an example of the design of a hard-skinned oven of about 425 °F in the baker ' s guide, approximately 491.5 K; this is two different readings from the actual temperature inside the bread.

What is it?

The bakery of bread is usually the air set in the oven, not the bakery. King Arthur's yeast baker guide recommends that hard-skin bread and food packs be baked quickly at 425 °F or more, Bentry takes 425 °F, about 218.3 °C, 491.5 K. [1][2] In the same guide, it was stated that long white bread centres could be released at approximately 190–195°F (approximately 87.8–90.6°C); the two were distinct objects. [1]

How?

The oven is set to be maintained by thermostats or electron controllers, and the actual cavity is affected by preheating times, opening doors, oven locations and hot wind cycles. To check the settings, independent thermoelectric dolls can be placed in the grill centre to record the air temperature after preheat stabilization. The bakery centre temperature is inserted from the side with a testimonial temperature measure, which stabilizes with the same reading; the guide gives white bread 190-195°F and whole grain bread approximately 200-205°F. [1] Examples of formulations also show that the same type of hard-skin bread can be used at 450 °F to indicate that the setting changes with the formulation and the furnace type. [2]

Value and Measurement condition

425 °F x 5/9 to 218.333 °C plus 273.15 to 491.483 K. 491.5 K is a fixed value conversion, not a temperature reached inside the bread; the white bread centre 190-195 °F only corresponds to approximately 361-364 K. The value of the oven shows a possible deviation of 10 K, especially for domestic ovens. [1] When comparing “temperature”, it must be stated whether it is set, cavity air, skin or centre.

Common misunderstandings

The most common misconception is that 425 °F is the internal temperature that must be reached as bread; in fact, the centre is much lower, and the colour on the outer shell is dependent on higher air typerature and surface water evaporation. The second misperception is that the height of the temperature is easier to cook; the temperature is too high to allow the skin to be fixed and the inside to remain alive. Third, 425 °F or 450 °F in the formulation is not a physical constant, but rather a choice of equipment and process; insufficient preheating, oven material and altitude change the result. Finally, the calibration of the oven is not equivalent to the tamperature reading.

Relations with other temperate areas

491.5 K is above the water pressure boiling point 373.15 K is also above the ethanol boiling point 351.5 K; it is almost the same as the smoke spot of fresh palm fluids of approximately 493 K, but one of the two is an equipment set and one is an oil sample test that cannot be directly equated. It is less than the melting point of aluminium 933.473 K and less than the surface of the paper under heat fire. The baking zone falls well within the context of the combined effect of “water boiling, oily smoke, food browning”.
