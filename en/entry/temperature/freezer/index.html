Freezer set temperature

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The safe storage target for the domestic freezer is 0 °F (−18 °C) or less, which is taken from −18 °C (255 K). It is a long-term storage target air attemperature, not freezing attemperature, or instant at food centres.

What is it?

Refrigerated room temperature usually refers to target air in the domestic freeze zone. USDA and FoodSafety.gov recommend that the freezer be kept at 0 °F (−18 °C) or below; this article takes −18 °C (255 K). [1][2] This is a security objective for long-term storage, not “frozen” temperature or the instant value of temperature at food centres. It's in two different storage compartments with 4°C in the freezer room.

How?

Refrigeration room temperature can be used either internally or independently; the vents and doors should be avoided at the point of measurement and given a steady reading after loading. According to USDA, food kept at 0 °F is reliable in terms of safety, but its quality continues to decline withtime. [1] FoodSafety.gov ' s storage form lists 0°F (−18°C) or below as a freeze condition and gives a recommended duration for different foods. [2] The frostless refrigerators are cyclically hotter, and the air is not constant, so readings should indicate the moment.

Value and Measurement condition

255 K equals −18.15 °C, approximately −18 °C. 0 °F strict conversion is -17.78 °C, so −18 °C is a round value. [1][2] The air in the freezer room fluctuates with frost cycles, opening doors and putting unfrozen food; the food centre is falling more slowly. Rapid freezing usually requires lower temperatures and stronger air currents, and domestic refrigeration rooms primarily guarantee long-term storage rather than instant freezing. A distinction should also be made between air, packaging surfaces and food centres.

Common misunderstandings

First, it is believed that −18 °C can kill all microorganisms; the freezing mainly causes them to go into hibernation and then to reproduce. Second, the set value is treated as food; reaching −18 °C inside large foods takes a long timetime. Thirdly, it is believed that the freeze will maintain its quality indefinitely; it is safe to keep it for a long time, but fat oxidation and freezing can affect the flavor. Fourth, the freezer is mixed with the freezer isemperature.

Relationship to other temperature

-18 °C is below 4 °C and 0 °C of water-freezing and below human-body-temperature and root-temperature. It is higher than absolute-zero, but less than about −10 °C and dry-ice-subscription about −78.5 °C. The temperature is at the intersection between domestic refrigeration and cryogenic projects: food is cold enough, but far from the liquid nitrogen or helium temperature zone.
