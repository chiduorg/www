What's this reading?

charm quark is the heavier of the second generation of quark, and the flow mass is about 1.27 GeV/c2, i.e.2.264×10⁻²⁷ kg. [1] This value is given at the 3 GeV scale, which is larger than the 0.938 GeV of Proton. So the strong ones with quarke (e.g. J/sau, D-gua) can no longer move quickly to decay by force, can only be weak, and their life is stretched to 10⁻¹² seconds order of magnitude.
