Itokawa

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The sum of Sasakawa (25143 Itokowa) is mass3.51×10¹⁰ kg, determined by the close-orbit tracking and shape model of the Hayabusa. It is about 535 metres long and has a density of about 1900 kg/m3, the first asteroid to be sampled back.

What's this reading?

The total mass of Sasakawa (25143 Itokowa) is 3.51×10¹⁰ kg, or approximately 35 million tons. [1] It is a near-Earth asteroid approximately 535 metres long, shaped like sea cucumber, and the target for the return of the Hayabusa sample. This number is the sum of mass of the whole celestial body, which consists of two contiguous gravel objects and does not contain a small amount of material that has been removed by solar wind and micro-impact.

Measureconvention with uncertainty

The article uses the general massmeasurement condition, which is set at level A relative to uncertainty by approximately 3 per cent. [2] Sasakawa has a very weak gravitational force and a small orbital disturbance of the detector, so its accuracy is less than one per cent better than that of Al-Fatron; however, it is still directly measured by gravitational force and is not an estimate of volume multiplier density. The average density of about 1900 kg/m3 mentioned by body text is also derived to describe the internal structure and not participate in massmeasurement.

How?

The Hayabusa stayed twice in 2005 at close range, most recently only a few kilometres from the surface, and radio ranging and optical navigation recorded the acceleration of the probe being pulled by Sichuan gravity; after deduction of solar light pressure and thermal radiation, it was proposed to combine the GM and obtain mass. [2] At the same time, the laser ranger and camera gave a shape model to determine the volume, and mass received density by volume. [3] The method is spacecraft tracking and orbiting, with a close-range suspension increasing the gravitational signal to the point where it can be met.

Order of magnitude

3.51×10¹⁰ kg is in the lowest segment of the B10 belt. [3] It is about half of Benu, about 1400 times lighter than Tututis, about 190,000 times lighter than the Star of Love; 12 times of magnitude compared to Moon 7.35×10²² kg. This mass has only about four parts a million of the Earth's surface gravity so that people can get out of the surface.

How do we keep going?

If you want to see its shape, surface gravel and samples, go to the space.asteroid-belt area of the space axis; if you want to compare another sample back to the target, go to Mass.asteroid-bennu; if you want to see the Dragon Palace of the Hayabusa II sample, go to space.ryugu.
