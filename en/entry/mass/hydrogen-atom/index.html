What's this reading?

Hydrotom is the lightest neutral atom: a proton, an Electron in 1s radical state. Its mass is approximately 1.67353×10⁻²⁷ kg, i.e. 1.007825 atomic mass unit. [1] This is the whole mass of neutral atom, which already includes Electron and includes losses from the combination of Electron and Proton.

Why not Proton and Electron?

proton mass plus electron mass are approximately 1.67353×10⁻²⁷ kg, which appears to be the same as hydrogen atom; however, a strict reduction would find hydrogenatom lightened by about 13.6 eV/c2, i.e. ZXXQPH1QZX kg. [2] The margin is the ionizing energy of hydrogen: taking Electron from 1s to the infinity of the energy needed to inject it, corresponding to a binding mass loss. The mass axis requires the use of atomic mass, rather than adding particles to it directly, for this reason.

How?

Hydroatom mass obtains 1.007825032 relative to atomic mass by atomic mass ruler and mass spectrometry, multiplied by atomic mass unit, i.e. kg. [1] Because hydrogen is the lightest atom, its mass is also one of the benchmarks for testing QED versus proton-electron mass; any deviation will influence the predictions of the Riddle constant and the atom spectrum simultaneously.

The PDG's particle mass table and NIST's atomic mass values are cross-referenced, and entry uses both public values to avoid a single source layouter. [3] The accuracy of hydrogen is also influenced by the proton-electron mass ratio, which is itself the subject of precision physics. [2]

Order of magnitude

The upper part of the B2 belt of 1.6735×10⁻²⁷ kg, which is with Proton and Neutron, is only one electron mass more than Proton. It is not a starting point for “macro matter”, but a starting point for chemistry and atom physics: from this point forward, molecules, cells and humans begin to emerge in stages.

How do we keep going?

To see its two components, go mass.proton and mass.electron; to see how helium binds four nuclears together, go mass.helium-nucleus; to see how the 21 centimetre spectrum of hydrogen marks neutral gases in universe, from space.hydrogen-21cm into space axis.
