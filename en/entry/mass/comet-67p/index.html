67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Rosetta is going to give 67P comet mass 0QX kg, about 10 billion tons, by the probe orbit. The density is only 533 kg/m3, which indicates that it is a comet of a high porosity pile, not solid ice.

What's this reading?

The total mass of 67P/Chulumov-Grassimenko is 9.982×10¹² kg, or approximately 10 billion tons. [1] This is the total mass of comet solids containing a mixture of aqueous ice, dust and organic matter; the gases and dust that have left the nucleotide in the comet and comet tail are not in it, and they are negligible at any time. Read this number bearing in mind that it is the whole mass given by gravity, not “volume times one hypothetical density”.

Measureconvention with uncertainty

This article uses the general massmeasure determination, and the source is the orbit of the Rosetta probe in the comet gravitational field, which is approximately three-tenths of a thousand. [2] If the same comet is estimated by a shape model multiplying the hypothetical density, it will receive a similar but not independent number; that would be tantamount to entering the density assumption as if it were larger and therefore not used in this article. 533 kg/m3 referred to in the text is the reverse spin-off of the mass divided by the shape modelvolume, not the premise of measration. [1] Measurement convention is used only once and is not mixed with comet mass and comet galaxy cons.

How?

In 2014, Rosetta arrived and spent a long time orbiting the comet nuclear, the probe was disturbed by the comet gravitational forces at different kernel intervals and accelerations in different orbits; these measurements, velocity data, combined with the back-effects of solar light pressure, comet gas injections, were designed to decipher the gravity parameter GM, divided by the gravitational constant G. [2] Close overflight and Philae lander location data have further tightened constraints. The method is spacecraft tracking and orbit integration, which is neither a scale nor radar ranging.

Order of magnitude

9.982×10¹² kg landed below the B10 belt. [3] It weighs about one hundred and thirty-six times more than Benu, five times less than Tutati, twenty-two times less than Harley; and twelve times of magnitude compared to 5.97×10²⁴ kg on Earth. The density is only about half the water, which means that the comet has a high pore rate, a pile of raw rubble that has not yet been contained, rather than a whole piece of ice.

If you want to see its shapes, surfaces and landing sites, go to space.comet-67p on the space axis; if you want to compare other small objects with the same mass, go to Mass.comet-halley, mass.asteroid-toutatis and mass.asteroid-bennu; if you want to understand the difference between mass and density, go to Mass.asteroid-itokawa.
