What's this reading?

Why is Proton stable?

Proton is the only stable free nuclear; standard model allows it to pass through a very rare process of decay, but the test gives a lower life limit than 10³⁴ order of magnitude. [2] This stability is not an “absolute proof”, but a constant tightening of the ceiling on observation. The stability of the proton is a prerequisite for the long-term existence of atom and the chemical world.

Read-ment convention and conversion

The quantity used for this article is 1.673×10⁻²⁷ kg, Measurement regulation is CODATA 2022 proton rest mass; combined by Penning zirconium and atomic mass rulers. Mass is wide and readings depend on wet/dry weight, blood/free of blood, package/full load, etc.; sample and measration conditions must be together. When comparing this reading to the adjacententry, replace it with the same unit and then take the margin; do not reduce it directly with a different value.
