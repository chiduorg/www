Eros

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Emerald (433 Eros) gross mass6.687×10¹⁵ kg, determined by NEAR Shoemaker ' s radio tracking and orbits, with a better accuracy than one per cent. It is the first near-Earth asteroid to be surrounded and weighed by a detector.

What's this reading?

The total mass of Eros is 6.687×10¹⁵ kg, about 6.7 trillion tons. [1] It is the first of the near-Earth asteroids to be surrounded and permanently tracked by a detector, so mass measurements are much more accurate than the object of the mine-only image. This number is the total mass, which contains the entire mass of an irregular stone object and does not involve any “volume multiplier density” estimation.

Measureconvention with uncertainty

This article uses the general massmeasurement condition, which is better than one per cent of uncertainty and is therefore classified as level A. [2] The small shape of the Star of Love is about 34 km long and, if it were to multiply the hypothetical density by volume, error would swing with the density hypothesis; the gravity of the NEAR avoids the problem. Body text refers to an average density of approximately 2670 kg/m3 which is inverted by mass divided by shape modelvolume, which is a by-product and does not participate in the security protection.

How?

NEAR Shoemaker went into orbit in 2000, after which more than a year of radio ranging, speed measurements recorded the acceleration of the detector in the gravitational field; after deduction of non-gravitational elements such as solar light pressure, albedo and thermal radiation, it was proposed to combine the gravitational parameters GM, and conversion to mass. [2] In 2001, it landed on the surface and the low-altitude tracking of the landing zone further tightened the constraints. The method is spacecraft tracking plus orbital integration, which is in the true sense of “orbital weighing”. [3]

Order of magnitude

6.687×10¹⁵ kg is in the upper middle of the B10 belt. [3] It is about six hundred and seventy times 67P, about one hundred and ninety times Sasakawa, about 130 times more than Tutati; it is still nine times of magnitude than the Earth5.97×10²⁴ kg. The mass of the Star of Love has come close to the lower limit of “difficultly gravitating to maintain the shape of a gravel by its own gravity”, but it is not enough to crush itself into a sphere.

How do we keep going?

If you want to see the shape and mission file, go to the space.asteroid-belt area of the space axis; if you want to compare the different methods of mass determination, go to mass.asteroid-itokawa and mass.asteroid-bennu (OSIRIS-REx); if you want to see radar values of only 20% accuracy, go to mass.asteroid-toutatis.
