How?

The Earth mass is determined mainly by the orbit tracking and gravity field coefficients of artificial satellites: the gravity to which the satellite is exposed is determined by the distribution of the Earth's mass and gravity field, and the distance and velocity changes of the ground station Measurement satellite, which are to be combined with the geogravity constant GM, divided by the gravity constant G. [2] The precision of this chain is limited by G's measurement precision, so the relative uncertainty of Earth mass is bigger than the GM itself. [3] This is also the reason why problems such as “Earth Multiple” may vary in detail in different sources.
