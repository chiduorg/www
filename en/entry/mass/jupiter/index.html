What's this reading?

Jupiter is the fifth planet in the solar system and the largest mass in the solar system, with more than the sum of the remaining seven. This article records its planetary total mass around 1.898×10²⁷ kg. [1] Measurement convention is the planetary mass that contains the atmosphere, the interior and the inner core and does not contain dark matter. It's about 318 times as much as Earth's mass, and it's a reference object for understanding giant planets.

Measureconvention with uncertainty

The total massmeasurement convention contains the atmosphere, the liquid and metal hydrogen internals and possible rock cores and does not include satellites and rings in the main mass. [2] Spacecraft tracking is about 0.02 per cent of the relative uncertainty given to the Galileo satellite orbit and is classified as level A. Jupiter mass is also often used as a measurement unit for other celestial bodies, and is frequently found in extraterrestrial research, such as Jupiter mass, a medical condition.
