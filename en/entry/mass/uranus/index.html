Uranus

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Uranus is the seventh planet of the solar system, with the planet mass around 8.681×10²⁵ kg, approximately 14.5 times the size of Earth mass. Mass is proposed to be combined with the satellite orbit by Traveller 2 overflight, which is approximately 0.02 per cent relative to uncertainty.

What's this reading?

Uranus is the seventh planet of the solar system and the first planet to be discovered with a telescope, with a rotation axis lying almost on the orbital surface. This article records its planetary total mass around 8.681×10²⁵ kg. [1] Measurement convention is the total mass of planets and contains the atmosphere, mantle and rock cores and does not contain dark matter. It's about 14.5 times the size of Earth's mass, ranking fourth among the four giant planets.

Measureconvention with uncertainty

General massmeasurement includes the atmosphere, ice mantle and rock core, but does not contain rings and satellites. The Traveler 2 overflight tracking is defined as level A, given approximately 0.02 per cent relative to the satellite orbit. [2] This precision is sufficient to support a comparison of the inner structure of the ice giant, so the body text retains four signatures; the number of digits is not meaningful to the reader.

How?

In 1986, Traveler 2 flew over Uranus and ground-controlled Measurement probes were subject to gravitational velocity changes, resulting in the determination of the mass of Uranus and the lower gravitational field. [2] The orbit of many of the planet ' s satellites is also dominated by planet mass, and the orbit cycle and shape of the long-term observation satellite can be independently verified. [3] Both paths confirm each other, making the mass values very stable and the basis for subsequent calendars.

Order of magnitude

8.681×10²⁵ kg is located on the lower end of B12 with approximately 85% of Neptune mass and 4.6% of Jupiter mass. [1] Uranus is close to Neptune mass, often referred to as ice giants, and their internal structure models are often compared together. It is about 6.5 times lighter than Saturn, about 14.5 times heavier than the Earth, and average density is only about 1.3 times more than water.
