Boeing 747-8 has an operational space of approximately 220,000 kg, containing airframes, engines, systems, interiors and unserviceable fuel, excluding payloads and recalcitrant fuel. The maximum take-off was approximately 447,700 kg, with a slight reduction of less than half.

What's this reading?

Boeing 747-8 has an empty weight of approximately 220,000 kg. The empty mass is the smallest mass that an aircraft can fly: airframe, engine, system, interior, seat and unusable fuel, excluding passengers, cargo and refuelling. [1] It has a maximum take-off of approximately 447,700 kg, with an empty weight slightly below half of this ceiling.

This reading describes “how much the aircraft itself is”, not “how much the aircraft can carry”. Combining it with the max is the most common misreading in aviation data.
