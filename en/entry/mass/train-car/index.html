High-speed rail car

The heavy-iron motorized fleet of single-hulled cars is made up of mass of approximately 40,000 kg, which contains aluminium alloy bodies, rotor frames, towed and brake equipment, air conditioning and seating and does not contain passengers. Different models and headers can be different to 40-55 t.

What's this reading?

One-way cars of the high-iron motorized fleet, equipped with mass of approximately 40,000 kg. This is the vehicle ' s car: aluminium alloy body, rotor rack, tractor and brake equipment, air conditioning, seating, window and contents, excluding passengers and luggage. [1] Multiple carriages are in a row, and the whole train mass is in hundreds of tons of order of magnitude.

This reading describes “a car”, not “a train”. The grouping, the front car, the motor vehicle with the electric bow and the transformer changes the single section mass; when reading any train parameters, make sure that it is a single section or a whole column.

The differences between the different types of vehicles are clear: the length of the car is about 25 m, and the lower axle is lower than 40 t, and the header and the equipment car are significantly heavier. [1][2] Section 40 t is therefore representative of this level; specific clusters are added by section according to the pattern table. Benentry takes 4.0×10⁴ kg as package mass.

There is also a distinction to be made between mass, post-carrying operations and axes. The package mass is empty; at 80-100 people per section, 65 kg per person, increases by 5-7 t, relative to 40 t less than 15%. The axes are assigned to each axis, which is the control of circuits and bridges. [2]

How?

Railway vehicles are weighed by orbital weights or by a vehicle upon departure from the plant and inspection, and are re-heavy by axle measurement wheel, which calculates the distribution of the whole car mass and the axle load; the axle must fall to the extent permitted by the route and the bridge, which is the direct reason why the body is lightened by heavy iron. [2] In its vehicle authorization, the EU Railways requires the declaration of mass, axle and wheel distribution as mandatory parameters for interoperability. [3]

Aluminium alloys, thin wall welding structures and decentralized equipment are designed to strike a balance between strength, air density and axis weights. The lighter the body, the same pull power can use more mass to carry passengers.
