Bennu

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Benu (101955) gross mass7.329×10¹⁰ kg, determined by OSIRIS-REx orbit tracking and gravity field, relative to uncertainty by about one thousand. The average density is approximately 1190 kg/m3, which is a very small pile of rubble.

What's this reading?

The sum of Benu (101955) is 7.329×10¹⁰ kg, or 73 million tons. [1] It is a carbon near-Earth asteroid with an average diameter of about 490 metres and a sampling target for OSIRIS-REx. This number is the sum of mass of the whole pile of gravel, which contains loose rock on the surface and internal solids, excluding the small amount of dust that has been thrown.

Measureconvention with uncertainty

This article uses the total massmeasurement convention, relative to the uncertainty of about one thousandth, which is of level A. [2] Benu shapes are irregular and rough, and if estimated by volume multiplier density, the results are swinged with the density assumption; the OSIRIS-REX gravity avoids this layer. The average density given by body text is about 1190 kg/m3 by mass divided by a shape of modelvolume, not by input.

How?

OSIRIS-REx arrived in Benu in 2018 and then, for more than two years, radio-tracking and optical navigation at a close range of about one kilometre in a radius; the gravitational acceleration of the probe, solar light pressure and thermal radiation were combined to decipher the gravitational parameters GM, and conversion to mass. [2] The mechanical arm and landing dynamics at the time of sampling also provide independent cross-checking. The method is spacecraft tracking plus orbit integration and is a standard approach to circular detection. [3]

Order of magnitude

7.329×10¹⁰ kg is at the bottom of the B10 belt. [3] It weighs about twice as much as Sasakawa, about 700 times less than Tututitis, about 136 times less than 67P; and twelve times of magnitude less than Moon 7.35×10²² kg. The density is only about 1.19 g/cm3, which is close to the water density, suggesting that the asteroid's inner voids are large volumes, a pile of debris bound by weak gravity.
