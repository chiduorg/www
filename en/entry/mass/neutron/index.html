What's this reading?

Neutron is a neutral partner of Proton, consisting of three valence quarks. Its rest mass is 1.67492750056×10⁻²⁷ kg, approximately 939.6565 MeV/c2. [1] Compared to Proton, neutron weighs only about 0.14 per cent, but this small margin is enough to change the direction of weak decay: free neutron will decay proton, not vice versa.
