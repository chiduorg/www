Electron

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Electron's rest mass are locked by Penning Zun with Atom in reverse measration, and CODATA 2022 gives9.1093837139×10⁻³¹ kg, relative to uncertainty about 3×10⁻¹⁰.

What's this reading?

Electron is a stable light with negative electricity and is silent as 9.1093837139×10⁻³¹ kg, approximately 0.511 MeV/c2. [1] The "quiet mass" here is not a variable mass: no matter how Electron moves, the number remains the same. The “mass increase” of the high-speed movement, often referred to in relativity, is only an increase in energy, not the subject of this record; the energy equivalent mass cannot be mixed into rest mass.

How?

Electron's mass is locked mainly through two separate paths. The first is the frequency of rotation in Penning, where the mass ratio is converted to mass using the known magnetic field; the second is the combination of toom's backlash with the Ryderberg constant, linking electron mass with the atomic mass ruler. [1] The two paths are consistent within the error range, and it is only CODETA that gives about 3×10⁻¹⁰ relative uncertainty. [2] This type of measurement does not rely on “heavy”, but instead transforms mass into a frequency ratio.

This frequency also makes electron mass a hub connecting the atom ruler to the basic constant, whereby CODATA updates the entire constant network every year. [2]

Where's Mass from?

In standard model, Electron's mass comes from a connection with Yukawa at Higgs. Combination strength is not a number that can be calculated, but a parameter that has to be entered in the experiment. [3] Therefore,9.109×10⁻³¹ kg is both a measurement value and a free parameter for model; it explains why Electron is five times of magnitude less than the top quark, but does not explain why the coupling takes this value.

Order of magnitude

Electron is the only particle in the B1 belt with a defined point value: it weighs about 10⁷ twice as much as the neutron text, 1836 times less than the proton. This ratio determines the size of the atom's scale: the smaller the electron mass, the larger the Bole radius, the larger the 1s of the hydrogen atom's cloud will be laid to 10⁻¹⁰ morder of magnitude.

Electron doesn't have a radius.

Electron is considered an spot particle in standard Model, and the experiment only gives a charge radius cap of about 10⁻¹⁸ miller of magnitude; this cap comes from dispersion and precisionmeasure, and does not mean that Electron actually has a small shell. Using an "electron radius cap" as a real radius, the meaning of a particle is misunderstood. [1][S1]

To see its anti-particle, go mass.positron; to see how it and Proton form hydrogen, go mass.hydrogen-atom and elements.h; to see how the conpton wavelength swaps mass for growth from space. Electron-compton into space axis.

Why is electron mass so accurate?

electron mass is not called as the scales, but converts mass to a frequency ratio: Penning Zero-System Frequency, atom Reedber Constant, and links the two to the Aatom mass ruler. [1][2] Frequency is extremely accurate, so the relative uncertainty of 10⁻¹⁰ order of magnitude is one of the most accurate in the CODATA constant network.

Ratio to proton mass

electron mass often appear in pairs with proton mass: proton/electron mass is approximately 1836.15, a pure number without units. [1][2] This margin determines the distribution of the Electron to the atom nuclear mass and affects the Bol radius and the atom energy level. It is more useful than remembering9.11×10⁻³¹ kg separately because the margin remains unchanged under different units. If the margin is significantly different, the chemical structure changes. CODATA placed the two masses in the same constant network precisely so that the margin could be independently tested.

Five-axis Contrast

On the mass axis, Electron is 10⁻³¹ kg of light; on the space axis, Compton wavelengths exchange mass for growth; on the temperature axis, electron mass does not directly give to temperature but determines the atom energy level and radiation; on the time axis, Electron is stable particles with extremely long life limits. "Mass Small" as "scale Small" would confuse the conpton wavelength with the classic radius. [1 [2][3]
