City bus

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The overall mass of the 12 m diesel city bus is approximately 12,000 kg and contains body, chassis, air conditioning, seat and all oil, excluding passengers. Pure electric vehicles of the same length usually weigh 2-3 t, with a maximum permissible gross mass of about 18-19.5 t.

What's this reading?

A spare mass of approximately 12,000 kg for a 12-m diesel mass bus. This includes body, chassis, engine, gear tank, air conditioner, seat, all oil and vehicle tools, excluding passengers. [1] It corresponds to the most common single bus size in city: about 12 m, two axes, low floor, able to stand.

This reading only covers "a 12 m diesel single machine." The numbers of attachments, double-storey vehicles and pure electric vehicles are not in this article; they belong to the same functional category but are different mass objects.

Measureconvention with uncertainty

The package of the same length bus varies considerably with the power pattern: diesel vehicles are about 11-13 t; pure electric power battery packages are usually weighed at 2-3 t; and 18 m pick-up vehicles can reach 18 t. [1] 12 t is the representative value of the 12 m diesel vehicle taken in this article, in grade A, as it corresponds to the manufacturer ' s whole car specifications, rather than being reversed by the passenger load.

The passenger capacity is derived from the margin: the maximum permissible gross mass is about 18-19.5 t, less the balance of 6-7 t. Based on 65 kg per person, approximately 90 to 100 people, this is the "full load" algorithm. [2] Using the master mass as a package mass, it is misread to read the "gravity" of 18 t. The classification and registration data for buses in Europe are also recorded in the respective columns for mass and gross mass. [3]

How?

The manufacturer weighs the amount of the axle load in the state of readiness and publishes the distribution of the front and back axle; both the public transport procurement contract and the annual inspection use this number as the axle load directly determines the tyre, brake and road load. [2] There will also be spot checks of axle loads to prevent overload.

The low-floor structure moved the equipment to the roof and back of the vehicle, making the axle-load distribution more sensitive than the traditional bus. Battery pattern also needs to be re-labelled by battery layout, which is the reason why the "front axle light" problem is common in electric bus traffic.

Order of magnitude

1.2×10⁴ kg equals approximately 9 Carola, or 2.4 head African elephants. It is more than three times lighter than a car moving, but it weighs more than the head of the truck; in the B8 belt, it represents “cityinfrastructure level mobile mass”. Mass is not the same as carrying passengers: a 12 t car can load nearly 100 people. [3]
