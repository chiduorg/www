Empire State Building

The operator of the Empire State Building declared the building to weigh 365,000 tons, approximately 3.65×10⁸ kg. The original text is written only tons, not indicated in tonnes, and the difference is approximately 10% if approximately 3.3×10⁸ kg is converted in United States short tonnes; the official tonnage is used in metric tonnes.

What's this reading?

The building mass declared by the operators of the Empire State Building was 365,000 tons, approximately 3.65×10⁸ kg. [1] This skyscraper built in 1931 was 381 m tall, with a total of 102 floors, using approximately 10 million bricks, 730 t aluminium and stainless steel, and approximately 5,700 m3 limestone and granite. This reading describes the entire building up from the top of the base, not the amount of the material used.
