Volvo FH16 tractor unit

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Volvo FH16 is equipped with approximately 9,000 kg, which refers to the head itself: chassis, cab, D16 diesel, speedbox, back bridge, saddle and fluid, excluding trailers and cargo. The long cab configuration is usually 8.5-9.5 t.

What's this reading?

The entire Volvo FH16 tractor is about 9,000 kg. This is its own mass: chassis, driver ' s room, D16 diesel engine, speedbox, back bridge, saddle seat and all of the fluid, with no trailer or cargo. It's a machine that can legally drag to 60-80 t combined mass, but it's only nine t.

The “head” and “train” must be clear first. The tractor is a tool, and the trailer is a reversible cargo container; mixing the mass of the two is five times less. This article contains only the header object.

Measureconvention with uncertainty

The configuration of the tractors follows the number of axes, the length of the cab, the volume of the tank and the position of the saddle: the long cab type of 6x4 often falls on 8.5-9.5 t, and the two tanks and the large-capacity cooling system push the numbers up the zone. [1] The manufacturer is published in terms of configuration, in grade A, as it comes from the weighing of the plant rather than from estimates.

Three volumes must be separated: packaged mass 9 t; maximum allowable gross mass for bicycles (approximately 18-26 t); maximum combination mass (total weight for trains 60-80 t). [2] To interpret “40 t truck” as a common error in head-to-head weight: 40 t usually means the total permissible weight of the load, which is more than four times different. European legislation on the registration and limitation of commercial vehicles is graded according to the same set of regulations. [3]

How?

Upon leaving the plant, the axle load is weighed, recording the front bridge, the drive bridge and the saddle load; the saddle load determines the weight to be assigned to the trailer and the grade of the toll station and the bridge. [2] It will be repeated at the time of listing and annual inspection that the retrofitting of the container and the fitting of the crane must be re-marked.

For tractors, the distribution of axle loads is more critical than total mass: insufficient capacity to drive a bridge loses pull, while the forward axle overloads affect the shift and brakes. This is also why the same car is changing the seat of the saddle, and the number on the pass changes.
