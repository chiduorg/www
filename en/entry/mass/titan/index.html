What's this reading?

Titan is the largest satellite of Saturn and the only satellite in the solar system with a dense atmosphere. This Article records its total mass of approximately 1.345×10²³ kg. [1] Measurement convention is the total mass of the celestial body, consisting of the rock inner core, the mantle, the liquid water layer and the dense nitrogen atmosphere, and does not contain dark matter.
