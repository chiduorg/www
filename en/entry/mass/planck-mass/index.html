Planck mass

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Planckmass m P = √ (ħc/G) ≈2.176434×10⁻⁸ kg from the approximate Planck constant, light speed and gravity constant of CODATA 2022. It is not a measurement mass of a particle, but a mass mass ruler whose gravitational effects have become immeasurable.

What's this reading?

Planck mass is about 2.176×10⁻⁸ kg or 21.8 ug. [1] It is neither the "minimal mass" nor the mass of a particle. Put around Planck constants, vacuum light speeds, c and Newton gravity constants, G-by-c/G, and the matrix is mass, right here. This is the combination given in the matrix analysis, not the scale reading.

Why do you need it?

Near Planck mass, a particle's Compton wavelength is similar to its Swazi radius, order of magnitude, and quantum mechanics and general relativism can no longer be used independently. [2] So it marks a "quantitative effect of gravity is not negligible" baseary, rather than a measurable object. universe does not have a rest mass of known basic particles approaching it: only about 3×10⁻²⁵ kg, at the height of 3×10⁻²⁵, 17 times of magnitude.

Measureconvention with uncertainty

This entry records the extrapolation value obtained by a combination of constants, not the point value of the event. CODATA gives 2.176434×10⁻⁸ kg, relative to uncertainty about 1×10⁻⁵, mainly from G-measurementorror, because ħ and c are much more precise. [1] Following the revision of the international unit system in 2019, the kg itself was inversely definition of the Plank constant,[3] which made Planck mass more direct in relation to the kilogram, but did not transform it into a quantifiable object. Its "accuracy" comes from formulas, which do not come from weighing; to discuss experimenteror, it goes to G.

Order of magnitude

2.176×10⁻⁸ kg lies at the lower end of the B6 belt: approximately ZXXQPH1QZX is greater than Proton, about eight times lighter than a 05 mm stone sand and about 100 times lighter than a mosquito. [2] On this axis, it is the only anchor identified by the constant definition rather than by the sample: it goes up into daily particles and goes down to particles and atom.
