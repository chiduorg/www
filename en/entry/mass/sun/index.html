How much is lost per second?

The sun transforms mass into energy by fusion, losing approximately 4×10⁹ kg per second; then it continues to eject substances via solar wind and radiation. This is a very small loss rate relative to the total mass of 2×10³⁰ kg but changes the star structure over billions of years. Mass is not an eternal attribute. [1][S2]

The solar mass also decreases with solar wind and coronal material ejection, but the loss rate is much lower than the loss of mass for fusion; in the red giant star phase, the mass loss will accelerate significantly. Now1.989×10³⁰ kg is the mass of the current sun, not the eternal value.

The solar mass can also reverse planetary orbits with the third law of Kepler: GM gills are much more accurate than G, so the solar mass error is mainly from gravitational constants.
