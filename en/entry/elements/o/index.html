Oxygen

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

From nitrogen to oxygen, 2p from 3 Electrons to 4; instead, the first ion energy dropped to 13.618 eV and the radius to 48 pm, 3.44 made oxygen the most vulnerable element in the second cycle.

Where did the eighth proton put Electron?

Oxygen on the right side of the nitrogen. The valence shell of nitrogen is 2s2 2p3, and the eighth electron of oxygen is still 2p, which is written in [He] 2s2 2p4. [1] Key changes are in pairs: there is already one electron in each of the three 2p orbits, and the fourth electron must be in the same orbit as one of the electrons, and some stabilization of the increase in nuclear charge is offset by the cologne exclusion of the pair. This led to the most notable anomaly of the second cycle: the radius continued to shrink from 56 pm of nitrogen to 48 pm, the electronegativity increased from 3.04 to 3.44, while the first ionizing energy dropped from 14.534 eV to 13.618 eV, reducing by 0.916 eV. [3] It is true that the nuclear charge has increased and that the effective nuclear charge is rising, but the electron exclusion in the same 2p orbit lowers the energy needed to lose an electron; for the first time, structural and energy change is moving apart.

Relative Z-1: Six Differences

  • First ionizing energy: 13.618 eV, 14.534 eV and 0.916 eV lower than nitrogen. [3]
  • Atom radius: 48 pm for oxygen, 56 pm for nitrogen and 8 pm for oxygen. [1]
  • Electro-negative: Oxygen 3.44 (Pauling scale), nitrogen 3.04 and oxygen 0.40 higher than nitrogen.
  • Valence electron configuration: 2s2 2p4 and 2s2 2p3 for oxygen; new Electron enters 2p and forms an Electron pair in one of the 2p orbits.
  • N/Z: A = 16 for oxygen, N = 8, N/Z = 1.00; N = 7, N/Z = 1.00 for nitrogen, 1 less than nitrogen.
  • Nuclear source: Oxygen is labelled as star helium combustion, nitrogen is labelled as CNO cycle; the main source of oxygen is changed from carbon nitrogen to helium combustion chain.

The main reason for these six is the double-up exclusion caused by the new electron on 2p: it keeps the first ion from rising and falling, the only one in six that needs to be read against the radius. The contraction of the radius 8 pm and the negative increase in electricity 0.40 are still associated with the increase in the effective nuclear charge; the three are not exactly in the same direction, which means that ionizing energy cannot be explained only by the radius. N/Z Maintain 1.00 is an arithmetical coincidence of A = 16, Z = 8; the conversion of nuclear source from the CNO cycle to star helium combustion indicates that the primary source of oxygen is another chain of nuclear reaction, with no causal link to changes in the electron layer.

Why does it matter?

2p4 allows oxygen to have two unopposed electron keys, the most direct example of which is water molecules; and negative electricity 3.44 makes oxygen second only to fluorine in the second cycle, making it easier to pull the shared electron to itself, so that oxides and oxygenated acids are particularly common in chemistry. [2] The oxygen molecule itself also retains a non-reciprocal Electron in the form of magneticity — the observable consequence of a 2p4 pairing, not a description of the purpose. Oxygen has three stable isotope 16O, 17O and 18O; unlike mass, they share the same 2p4 valence shell. [2] Placing oxygen on the element axis means that for the first time a monotonous rise in ionizing energy was interrupted with a pair of exclusions.

Where did it come from?

Oxygen nucleus is marked as star helium burning, a clue that hangs at the time. first-stars node of the Time axis. [1] During the helium combustion phase, the 12C that has been generated captures a 4he nuclear into 16O; the ratio of oxygen to carbon depends on the temperature, density and persistence of helium combustion, so that the carbon ratio given by Star is not the same. The abundance of oxygen is high because helium combustion not only makes carbon, but also continues to process carbon into oxygen, which itself captures 4he to the heavier core. After the first generation of Stars ran the chain, oxygen became one of the heavy elements common in the interstellar medium. [2]
