Closed shell 86: radon and the filled 4f5d6s6p

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

For the first time in the sixth cycle, 14 positions of 4f were inserted, and the lanthanide series contracted to tighten 6s electron. Radon is the end of this long cycle and the only closed shell that is largely supplied by radioactivitydecay chain.

Where did this floor end?

The order of filling for the sixth cycle is 6s & 4f & 5d & 6p. Cesium, first 6s, then 4f, luteium to mercury 5d, radon 6p. [2] Radon's configuration [Xe] 4f145d106s26p6, with a first ionation energy of about 10.7 eV, lower than a thorium. It's special not on the valence shell, but on the inner level: 4f 14 Electron's shield against the nuclear charge is very incomplete, resulting in a much smaller atom radius than expected for the sixth cycle, which is the lanthanide series shrink. [1]

Why does the next floor start here?

Electron enters 7s. The first ionizing power of francium is about 4.1 eV, close to caesium, suggesting that 7s electron is similarly shielded by the inner layer. [2] Next to 7s, we have to fill 5f and 6d, actinide servicees inserted here to extend the seventh cycle to 32 elements. Once again, the shell becomes “the starting point of the next complex structure” rather than a clean end.

Where do these elementes come from?

Radon exists almost as a direct product of the star nuclear synthesis, mainly from the intermediate steps of uranium-238, uranium-235 and thorium-232. [3] Radon-226 releases alpha particles and turns to radon-222, and radon continues decay. Thus, Radon's “source” is not a one-off manufacture of unversescale, but a continuous nuclear decay inside the Earth. The clue should be on the time axis at the 238 half-liffe node, not on the supernova node.
