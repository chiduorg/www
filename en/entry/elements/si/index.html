Where did 14 Proton put Electron?

Why does it matter?

The four valence Electrons of Silicon tend to form a four-directed co-valent key, and the crystals are a four-sided network, rather than the Electron Sea in metal. [2] This network binds Electron to a key, and it crosses the energy gap to conduct electricity, so that silicon becomes a semiconductor: the conductive capacity is controlled by mixing and temperature, rather than by the amount of free Electron. Three stable Sotope 28Si, 29Si, 30Si show that 14 protons can be matched with different numbers of neutrons, and that tolerance at the nuclear level is different from “just four keys” at the electron level. Aluminium and silicon are seen in parallel, and the recovery of ionizing energy is not a gradual loss of Electron, but a shift in the form of a key from the "shared Electron Sea" to the "directed covalent".

Where did it come from?

Silicon source writes "Oxygen and Silicon Combustion". Oxygen combustion converts oxygen into silicon and sulphur-like nuclides, while silicon combustion takes place in the final stages of larger massstar evolution, with a very high core of temperature, photolysis taking place at the same time as the nuclear reset, and the products approaching the iron peak. The first generation of Stars and Supernova nodes on the time axis describe the process by which the product was thrown back into the medium by supernovas, increasing the intensity from generation to generation. Silicon universe has a high abundance, not because it is particularly stable, but because both routes of oxygen combustion and silicon combustion lead to it; by contrast, aluminium relies on fragmentation supplement, phosphorus relies on neutron to capture the secondary road, with its share naturally being pulled apart. Once silicon combustion is activated, only a short time in the core collapses, which also limits the share of more than silicon.
