Lanthanide contraction and relativistic effects

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

4f electron is shielded against each other, leading to the overall contraction of the sixth cycle; the inner Electron speed is approaching light speed, further tightening the 6s orbit. Mercury at constant temperatures is liquid, gold yellow, platinum chemical inertity.

4f.

4f The shape of the orbit is more dispersed, the mean distance between Electron is larger and the efficiency of shielding is low. For each additional proton, the addition of 4f electron could not effectively offset the increase in the nuclear charge, so the effective nuclear charge continued to rise, with the atom radius and the ion radius shrinking. [1] From zirconium to zirconium, the trivalent ion radius fell from about 1.06 to about 0.85 Egypt. This range, which does not appear to be significant, is sufficiently similar to the chemical properties of eliment and changes the size of the latter.

How the contraction is transmitted to the element in the back.

The effect of the contraction is more than the contraction itself. The ion radius of the sixth cycle is almost the same as that of the fifth cycle, precisely because 14 4f electrons were inserted in the middle, and the ion radius was pushed back to levels close to the thallium. [1] This explains why thorium and thorium are almost always symbiotic in nature, and why similarities of a homogenous nature give rise to “intercyclical” anomalies. When comparing homologues, the eliment page must consider the inner layer fill instead of the primary quantum number.

The effect of relativity increases contraction.

The Electron velocity near the core is close to the speed of light, and the mass increases, s orbital radius shrinks and energy declines; this change is also influenced by shielding of the p, d, f orbit. [2] For the sixth cycle, 6s of shrinkage has placed 6s2 Electron of mercury in an abnormally tight position, resulting in weak metal keys and liquid mercury at constant temperatures. The 5d and 6s of gold are reordered by relativity effects, absorbing blue light and yellow; the 6s of platinum shrink increases its chemical inertia. These are not direct predictions of “ethnic trends”.

How it changed the way you read the element page.

When reading element for the sixth and seventh cycles, the main quantum cannot be extrapolated only. The “relative Z-1” column on the element page is followed by a special indication of changes in the inner layer: the new electron may enter the 4f rather than the valence shell, and the radius may be in the opposite direction as the homogenous trend. When the properties of thorium, thorium and tantalum, molybdenum and tungsten are seen to coincide almost with the nature of element, the condensation should be thought of; when the anomalies of mercury, gold and platinum are seen, the relativity effects should be thought of.

Common Misunderstanding and Boundary

The first misperception is that the contraction is considered to be particularly small; in fact, the contraction is continuous and moderate, with only one small step, and a cumulative 14 steps are significant. The second misconception is that all relativities of element are attributed to the relativity effect; the relativity effect is indeed important, but the crystal structure, the electron connection, and the pacing environment are equally involved. [3] The third misunderstanding is to treat the projection as a real one: the contraction of excess element is mainly calculated, experimental data are scarce and the element page must indicate the predictive properties.
