What is it?

There are two main pathways for artificial synthesis. In the reactor, the uranium nuclear absorbs neutron and then transforms itself into neutron, plutonium, americium, americium; in the accelerator, the light core accelerates to a sufficient level of energy, blasts the target core, and the two are integrated into a heavier core. [1] Post-104 element is almost entirely dependent on the latter, and a single experiment may produce only a few atoms, with survival time ranging from seconds to milliseconds.

How?

Identification of the new element is based on the Decay chain: the new core releases alpha particles, the core releases alpha particles, the energy of each step is comparable to the fingerprint of half-life and eventually links to a known nuclide. [1] Repeat experiments and independent validation are prerequisites for IUPAC to identify element. [2] In the case of overweight element, the theoretical prediction of “stabilized islands” remains the direction of the study, but it cannot be regarded as a fact of opservation.

Common misunderstandings

Artificial synthesis is not equivalent to “universe does not exist”. Another misperception is that the nature of overweight element is already being measured: most elements after 109 do not have experimental values, but only predict or leave empty. [1] Synthesizing an atom does not amount to “discovery”, which requires independent repetition, closure and official naming.
