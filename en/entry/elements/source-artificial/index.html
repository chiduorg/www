What is it?

There are two main pathways for artificial synthesis. In the reactor, the uranium nuclear absorbs neutron, then by β decay, becomes neptunium, plutonium, americium; in the accelerator, the light core is accelerated to a sufficiently high level of energy to strike at acinide series, and the two are integrated into a heavier nuclear.
