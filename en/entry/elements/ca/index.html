Calcium

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The calcium fills 4s into a pair, and 4s is still under 3d; the ionization jumps, the radius shrinks, the calcium stabilizes at two prices, and 3d does not start filling until the calcium is filled, and it is the last master of the 3d gate.

Where did 20 Proton put Electron?

The valence shell of potassium is 4s1, and the calcium excess electron still enters 4s, replacing it with 4s2, with [Ar] 4s2. [1] 3d continues to be empty, so calcium is still dominant on Electron's canvas: the outermost layer is a pair of 4s Electron, and the inner layer is the complete core of the americium. There is no re-orbital re-alignment, and there is no anomaly; this is because the power difference between the fours and the threed has not been offset by the exchange of the threed.

Compared to potassium, 4s Electron has changed from single to one, and exclusion in the same orbit has slightly raised this pair, but 4s is still below 3d. The shield structure has not changed and the effective nuclear charge has increased only 1, so the contraction of the radius is mainly due to an increase in the nuclear charge rather than to the opening of the new shell. The calcium rule is temporary: next proton, 3d will start receiving Electron.

Relative Z-1: Six Differences

  • First ionizing energy: calcium 6.113 eV, potassium 4.341 eV, increase 1.772 eV. [3]
  • Atom radius: calcium 194 pm, potassium 243 pm, decrease of 49 pm (calculated value medical condition). [1]
  • Electricity negative: calcium 1.00, potassium 0.82, increase 0.18.
  • Valence electron: Potassium 4s1 and calcium 4s2; new Electron enters the same 4s orbit and 3d remains empty.
  • N/Z: calcium-40 has 20 neutrons and potassium-39 has 20; Proton has 1 increase and mass has 1 increase, and the number of neutrons remains unchanged. The calcium consists of six stable isotope 40Ca, 42Ca, 43Ca, 44Ca, 46Ca, 48Ca, of which 48Ca half-life is approximately 6×10¹⁹ years.
  • Nuclear source: Potassium is classified as “explosive oxygen combustion and supernova”, calcium as “silic combustion and supernova”, and source is transferred from explosive oxygen combustion to silicon combustion.

The main reason is the increase in the nuclear charge and the “what left behind by the loss of Electron”: the loss of potassium 4s1 and the special stability of the product resulted in low ionizing energy; and the loss of a 4s Electron with only 4s1 left without the return of the shell, so the energy is high. 4s Optimal exclusion is a secondary factor, allowing for no greater increase. The reduction in the radius of 49 pm is a direct result of the nuclear charge; the constant number of neutrons is only the arithmetical result of adding 1 to the number of mass and the number of proton. The shift of source from explosive oxygen combustion to silicon combustion indicates that calcium is closer to the production range of static combustion.

Why does it matter?

4s2 makes calcium typical binary: when two 4s electrons are lost, this product is energy-efficient; the third electron has to move to 3d at a steep cost, so the chemistry of calcium almost runs around this double electron. It doesn't end up handing over an Electron like potassium, nor does it start using 3d as it did later on. Because 4s electron is loose and 3d is not involved, calcium compounds are predominantly ionist and its role is close to a binary centre.

The second thing is on the nuclear side. The half-life of 48Ca is about 6×10¹⁹, which is almost stable in practice;[2] its 20 protons and 28 neutrons fall on the number of shells and therefore remain extremely difficult to decay, albeit far from the neutronproton median. The contrast between the isotope family of calcium, which is close to the middle line, and the 48Ca, which is a closed shell “continuation”, suggests that stability is not determined only by N/Z.

Where did it come from?

Soundertry is listed as “Silica Burning and Supernova”. Calcium falls between the products of Silicon burning: Silicon burning pushes nuclides in the direction of iron peaks, an earlier station on the chain, followed by a core collapse to throw the whole layer of matter out. Corresponding to the time axis, its main contribution came from the supernova outbreak of the third generation Star, not from the original nucleination; the original nuclei produced little in the mass number of calcium. The calcium path is closer to the end of the electrostatic silicon combustion than the “explosive oxygen combustion” of potassium, and the output is thus more stable.

The logic of abundance here is that calcium can be steadily manufactured by silica burning, so its stocks accumulate with the star generation, rather than with the special conditions of a single outbreak; it is still not the end, and the real end is iron. The next generation, Star, went through this process repeatedly, injecting calcium into the interstellar medium, a build-up recorded at the concentration nodes of element.

How do we keep going?

Looking back at potassium (/entry/elections/k/), you can see why the move from 4s1 to 4s2 allows ionization to jump; looking forward at titanium (/entry/elections/ti/), you can see how 3d starts filling the later radius to continue contraction. You want to find out the energy order of 4s and 3d, read the energy explanatory page; you want to put 48Ca long half-life back to Protonneutron and read the nuclear stability interpretation page. The significance of calcium is that it is the last "clean" master of the 3d gate.
