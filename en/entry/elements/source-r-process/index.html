The r-process in Neutron-Star Mergers

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The r process pushes the nuclei away from the stabilizer line by very high neutron flux, and the beta decay brings them back to the stabilizer line. A significant part of the Europium, gold, platinum, uranium, thorium comes from this path and neutron is the most direct candidate.

What is it?

R The process is “fast” neutron capture: high neutron flux is shorter than beta decay, the nuclides are pushed quickly to the distance of the neutron surplus, and then back to the stabilization line by beta decay as the projectile swells and the temperaturee drops. [1] The products cover heavy cores from near the iron peak to actualide series, including Europium, zirconium, gold, platinum, uranium, thorium. The most direct candidate is a combination of two neutrons.

How?

GW 170817 in 2017 provides key evidence: after the gravitational wave signal, the brightness and colour evolution of the optical counterpart AT 2017gfo corresponded to the radioactivity decay of the rich neutron projector, with heavy element features in the spectrum. [2] Star archaeology also provides circumstantial evidence: the r process abundance model of the very poor metal star is comparable to the sum of the combined production. Uranium-232 and thorium-232 used r process products as “clock clocks”.

[2] Therefore, the concentration of r processes is repeated throughout the history of universe, with the solar system inheriting multigenerational mixtures. Common misunderstandings

The juxtaposition of neutron is not the only candidate, and models such as the Magnetic Rotation Supernova are still under discussion; all r processes are overdetermined. [1] Gold is not “only from a combination” and its output is combined with event rate and observation statistics. Another misconception is that the r process is seen as the explosion itself: the true neutron capture took place in a short window where the projectile swells.

Relationship to other phases

R Processes complement s Processes: s Processes slowly climb along the stabilization line, r Processes go around the outside of the stabilization line and come back. [2] Both explain the abundance of heavy element in the solar system and provide natural stocks to actinides.
