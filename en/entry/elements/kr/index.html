4p5 of bromine to 4p6 of krypton: last Electron fills 4p, ionization rises to 14.000 eV, radius shrinks to 88 pm. 4s3d4p 3rd leg completed and Krypton became the fourth cycle closed-hull node.

Where did 36 proton put Electron?

The valence shell for bromine is 4p5, and one more proton for krypton is then [Ar]3d10 4s2 4p6. [1] Add Electron to 4p to fill the last empty slot; and close all of this at 4s, 3d, 4p. Compared to bromine, the nuclear charge of krypton increased by 1, but the 4p has closed down, there are few low-energy key channels through which Electron can walk, and the first ionizing power rises from 11.814 eV to 14.000 eV, adding 2.186 eV. [3]

4p Electron is in the outermost layer, 3d10 is shielded incompletely and therefore the effective nuclear charge is higher; the radius is reduced from 94 pm to 88 pm. Kryptonics are not "all fourth-energy layers filled" because 4d is empty; it ends with 4s3d4p, the valence shell. The fourth cycle was closed here precisely because the three paragraphs were completed sequentially and 4d were postponed to the fifth. Krypton's shell is thus more “completed” than its aluminum, and it shows more clearly the rhythm of “closed shell - next cycle alkali metal”.

  • Electro-negative: Krypton 3.00, bromine 2.96, up 0.04.

Reduced radius 6 pm and increased ionizing energy are two manifestations of change in the same configuration. To look at all three together, it is possible to avoid the simple equivalent of "closed shell" to "highest negative electrical power".

Why does it matter?

Crusts make Krypton almost uninvolved in chemical reactions under normal conditions: valence shell 4p6, free of space, loss of Electron to pay 14,000 eV for ionizing energy, and access to Electron only enters a higher 5s or 4d. [2] But “inertity” is not absolute. Krypton can still form fluoride under conditions of enhanced oxidation, indicating that 4p Electron is not completely non-usable; it is normal reaction, not zero, of the chemical possibility, that the shell is reduced. It is therefore appropriate to distinguish between “othermodynamics stability” and “dynamic inertia”: the former are low-energy and the latter are reaction paths blocked.

Krypton or fourth cycle closed hub: one Electron to the left, fives to the right, fifth cycle. It has six stable isotope, of which 86Kr's number of neutrons is 50, which happens to be the illusion of the hull; chemically, the 4p shell appears in the same cell as the nuclear N=50 shell, but the two are different shell mechanisms. Where did it come from?

Facts label krypton nuclear source as "s processes and r processes". This means that Krypton has two complementary paths: one is the slow capture of neutron in the giant star, which increases along the stabilization line, and the other is the fast-track capture of neutron in combination with supernova or neutron, which returns when it is created far from the stabilization line. [1] Krypton is not a legacy of the original synthesis, nor is it a direct result of the burning of iron peaks; it must continue to move up in an environment that already contains heavy seed nuutrons.
