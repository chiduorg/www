Carbon

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

From boron to carbon, 2p from 1 Electron to 2; 11.261 eV ionizing energy, 67 pm radius and 2.55 electronegativity, allowing carbon to build stable covalent skeletons with four valence Electrons.

Where's the sixth proton put Electron?

The carbon atom core has six protons. By filling order, the first two Electrons go 1s, followed by two go 2s; by boron 2p there is 1 Electron, and the sixth carbon Electron continues to go 2p, with [He] 2s 2 p2. [1] The new electron does not enter 2s because 2s are fully staffed, while 2p is in the second layer of energy with 2s; it does not jump to the third level because 3s is too high. The third proton brings an increase in the nuclear charge greater than the additional 2p electron shield, so the effective nuclear charge rises from 87 pm of boron to 67 pm. [3] The structural change of this step appears to be just one more electron on the 2p, which actually pushes carbon to a tetravalent position: 2s22p2 in total of four electrons, which can be fully associated with the key and can be redistributed by 2s in combination with 2p.

Relative Z-1: Six Differences

  • First ionizing energy: Carbon is 11.261 eV, boron is 8.298 eV, and carbon is 2.963 eV above boron. [3]
  • Atom radius: 67 pm carbon, 87 pm boron, 20 pm less carbon than boron. [1]
  • Electricity negative: Carbon 2.55 (Pauling scale), boron 2.04 and carbon 0.51 above boron.
  • Electron configuration for valence: carbon of 2s2 2p2 and boron of 2s2 2p1; new Electron enters 2p, making Electron of 2p from 1 to 2.
  • N/Z: Number of mass A = 12 for carbon, based on N = A-Z obtained N = 6, N/Z = 1.00; number of neutrons equal to 6 for boron A = 11, Z = 5, N = 6, N/Z = 1.20.
  • Nuclear source: Carbon is marked as star triα; boron does not have a source field in the data sheet to give a comparable path to carbon.

In these six cases, the main cause of the increase in nuclear charge was the change of the effective nuclear charge with the excess of Electron at 2p: a radius contraction of 20 pm, an increase in ionizing power of 2.963 eV, and a negative electricity rise of 0.51, all accompaniing this primary cause. The number of neutrons remains the same as the number of adjoining masses, which cannot be written for structural reasons; the nuclear source jumps from the Electron layer to the nucleo-synthetic layer, and the carbon triple-alpha process does not have the same source field as the boron, and the two cannot be the same.

Why does it matter?

Four valence electrons are the most important structural fact of carbon: 2s2 2p2 can all input into the key and form four covalent keys; 2s is close to 2p energy and can be combined into a combination of sp3, sp2, sp, and so on, allowing carbon to be linked, ring and multiple keys. [2] Negative power 2.55 is medium, so carbon is not a strong grab on electron, nor is it a strong loss on electron, which is best suited to the common electron for building the skeleton. Its two stable isotope are 12C with 13C, 14C with 5,700 years of half-life; the latter allows carbon-containing samples to be accounted for by radioactivity decay, but it is nuclear, not four-valence source. [2] Carbon is important because the possibility of four keys is opened as soon as the valence shell moves from 2p1 to 2p2. Without these four valences, the chain of carbon and the ring skeletons cannot be mentioned.

Where did it come from?

The carbon nuclear source is marked as the Star Three Alpha process, a thread that hangs at the time.first-stars node of the time axis. [1] Three 4H nuclears met in the Star Helium core, first synthesising the highly unstable beryllium intermediate core, then capturing a third 4H in a very short time window, forming 12C; without this, helium burning would have been cut off at this light nuclear gap. The first generation ran the path, carbon entered the interstellar medium, and later the star and planets had carbon. The abundance of carbon is not so much due to the original synthesis of direct carbon, but to the way that the star helium burning has found a way across this light nuclear gap. [2]

How do we keep going?

It looks like boron, it's not in the A-grade intestry; then it's lithium, and it shows how 2s hook up. One step forward is nitrogen, 2p from 2 Electron to 3. To see how 2s and 2p are mixed into four keys, why carbon can form so many homologues, go to Electron's Fabrication Page (/entry/elections/explain-configration/). The error book of this value.

Carbon atomic number Z = 6, atomic weight around 12.011, is a weight of abundance of 12C and 13C. 12C happens to be the baseline of the atomic mass unit u, so its atomic mass definition is 12 u; but carbon weight is not 12 because it has isotope. Consider carbon = 12 as atomic weight and ignore abundance.

Another scale to look at it.

Carbon is the skeleton of life. It is the common element of steel, plastics, diamonds and graphite. Both diamonds and graphite are carbon-synthetics, and the difference between the hardness and the conductivity comes from the type of key coupling, not from the identity of element. The carbon cycle connects the atmosphere, oceans, organisms and rock, and is key to understanding climate and energy.
