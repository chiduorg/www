Energy levels, subshells and the shape of the periodic table

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The shell is not a concentric circle, but an orbit where a group of energy is close. S, p, d, f capacity differentials and penetrations determine how many elements can be placed in each line, and 4s and 3d in order.

The shell is not the same as the substrate.

The “electron layer” in secondary school textbooks is the approximation of the number of primary quantum n: the larger the n, the farther electron average is from the core. Within the same layer, however, the number of angular quantum l is different in orbital energy, and is divided into s, p, d, f substrates with two, six, ten, fourteen. [1] There are only 1s on the first level, 2s and 2p on the second level and 3s, 3p and 3d on the third level. The seventh step of the element axis is the direct result of this group of capacity.

The ability to penetrate determines the energy order.

The probability of s Electron's distribution in areas close to the nuclear site still has a large share, and this “penetration” allows it to feel a higher effective nuclear charge and lower energy pressure. The p, d, f penetrating is subdued, and the energy is elevated. [2] So energy sequences are usually lower than np and lower than nd and lower than nf in the same number. When the substrate energy of the different primary quantum numbers approaches, there is an interlocking of 4s below 3d, resulting in long and short lines.

Why is it 2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, 32 in each row?

Cuts the sublevel capacity in fill order, and the length of the cycle is obtained. In the first line, there are only 1s, 2s, 2s, 2p, 8; 3s, 3p, 8; 4s, 4d, 4p, 18; 18 in the fifth line; 6s, 6s, 4f, 5d, 6p, 32; and the same 32 in the seventh line. [3] These figures are not artificial but are the result of a combination of capacity and energy sequences. The shape of the periodic table is essentially a layout that fills the scale.

How it changed the way you read the element page.

The “group” is no longer merely a vertically aligned label, but a collection of “same valence forms”; the “cycle” is no longer just a row, but a “same sublayer is being filled” phase. When you see iron in the eighth, you think it's 3d with 4s; when you see kryp in the eighteenth, you think 4p is just filled. The "New Electron into which sublevel" in the element page asks which sublevel is relevant only in the context of a capable image.

Common Misunderstanding and Boundary

The first misperception is to think of Electron as a small ball running along a fixed orbit; the correct image is the probability density, the so-called “filling” is the least energy occupation, not the queue on the space. The second misconception is that the order of power is fixed for all elements; the reordering of relativity with electron ostracism is particularly evident as it increases. [2] The third misunderstanding is to treat “substrate capacity” as a “chemical valence”; the capacity is the orbital factor multiplied by two and the valence is determined by the actual loss, sharing or acquisition of Electron, which cannot be directly converted.
