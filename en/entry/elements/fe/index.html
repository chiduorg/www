Where did 26 Proton put Electron?

The valence shell of manganese is 3d5 4s2, and the iron excess electron enters 3d and gets [Ar] 3d6 4s2. [1] This is the first time that 3d continues to be filled on a half-filled basis: in five 3d orbits, one was forced to host a second Electron, half full of broken systems, and the system worked against each other. 4s remain fully staffed and did not participate in this re-alignment.

The shielding of this step is subtle: the new 3d electron is in the same layer, and the shielding of the outermost 4s is not complete, so the effective nuclear charge rises more markedly than that of the main family element. As a result, iron ' s first ionation continues to rise and the radius continues to shrink, but in a smaller range than that of manganese. The valence shell of iron is no longer a “half full and beautiful configuration”, but a configuration that must be redistributed by key.

The main reason is 3d pair: the first 3d Electron of iron is half full of broken iron, and pairs offset some of the increase in nuclear charge, so the increase in ionizing energy is 0.469 eV smaller than 0.667 eV of manganese. The negative electricity rise 0.28, the largest in this paragraph, suggests that 3d6 makes atom more attractive to elecron, which is the two sides of the same coin as "half full of broken, the system more inclined to redistribute elecron by key." The constant number of neutrons is the result of the simultaneous increase in the number of masses and the number of protons; the word “peak of iron” is the judgement given by the synthesis, not the configuration.

Why does it matter?

3d6 4s2 leaves four pieces of iron unmatched 3d Electron, and iron atom is therefore magnetic; in solids, the exchange between these 3d Electrons also allows iron to show iron magnetism. [2] But iron is really special at the nuclear level: each nuclear in the vicinity of the iron peak can be at its highest level, and neither fusion nor fission will release energy. So, once the core of Star is pushed to iron by silicon combustion, the energy supply breaks, and the core collapses only under gravity — that's the starting point of the supernova outbreak.

Let's put these two things together, the 26th iron proton recast the Electron layer and Star Destiny: 3d pairs make its chemistry variable and magnetic, making its core the end point of the fusion chain. The iron is discussed on the delement page, and if we don't talk about “why do we stop here” there is only one table of nature.

Where did it come from?

The iron is described by sourcecentry as “silon burning endpoints and supernovas”. That's not a saying: Silicon burning chains reach the end of energy near iron peaks, continue fusion to suck energy, so iron accumulates at the core until the core collapses, supernovas throw out the outer layer. [1] On the time axis, the third-generation supernova node is the main export of iron into the interstellar medium; the original nucleo-synthesis does not produce iron at all, and the iron in universe is a generation of stars burning and blowing up.

In abundance, iron peaks mean that the products of silicon burning will accumulate here, rather than continue to burn: the products of the first step are being sent in and the latter is almost non-existent, and a peak is formed. The next generation, Star, has mixed this iron into the interstellar medium, and it is the accumulation process recorded in the concentration nodes of element; that is why the iron source page must be accompanied by two supernovas and concentrations, not just half of them.

How do we keep going?

Looking back at manganese (/entry/elections/mn/), you can see why the half-filled 3d5 4s2 was broken here in iron; looking forward at cobalt (/entry/elections/co/) you can see how element after the iron peak is made by supernovas rather than by static combustion. To put iron peaks back in Star Energy, to read third-generation Star supernova nodes; to look at how element accumulates from generation to generation, to read element nodes. Iron is the watershed on the element axis: before it, fusion is energy; after it, fusion is consumption.

Another scale to look at it.

Iron is the main component of the earth's core and the basis of steel. The Earth's magnetic field is created by the convection of the external nuclear liquid iron; the blood protein is combined with oxygen by iron atom. Compared to carbon, iron fusion into iron peaks releases energy, and post-iron element requires the capture or explosion of neutron.
