Electron–Positron Annihilation

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The numbers of early electrons and positive electrons decreased significantly with cooling and energy and intropy were redistributed to particle hot baths. This is an ongoing process with a clear physical basis and cannot be crushed into a second when all particles disappear simultaneously.

What is it?

Electron versus positive Electron in early plasma. At high temperatures, photons can be paired to produce electrons -- positive electrons, and electrons can be neutralized into photons, which maintain a chemical balance.
