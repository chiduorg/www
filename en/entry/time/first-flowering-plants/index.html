Early Cretaceous Flowering Plants

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Some 130 million years ago, Fossil records provided clues to the early history of flowering plants. Their subsequent expansion has changed many terrestrial ecolory systems, but Fossil cannot simply be equated with the moment when the entire plant was actually born.

What is it?

The flowering plants (sorted plants) reproduce with flowers, fruits and closed embryos. The earliest reliable big Fossil records were found in the early dawn, around 130 Ma, and since then they have expanded rapidly in the terrestrial ecology system. [1] Flowers and fruits are not suddenly integrated, and the organ forms of the early members differ significantly from the current class. The identification of the phyto plant also relies on characteristics such as the packaging of embryos in fruit, double infertiles and trituitive pollen; the single foliage shape can easily be confused with some naked plants. [2]

How?

The evidence included leaf prints, pollen, fruit and flower fossil. Leaves and pollen that can be classified into the sub-plants have appeared in the sediments of the early and white world, and researchers have distinguished skincells, airholes and leaf veins from naked plants. [1] Molecular symmetry also gives origin time estimates, but it relies on model and calibration points and cannot replace Fossil evidence. [2] The form of pollen from the earliest phytophylls is relatively simple, and researchers usually combine multiple organs rather than a piece of the voucher to identify them. [2]

Time Coordinates and Measurement condition

About 130 Ma is in the early white world. According to the International Stratigphy Commission, version 2024/12, the lower part of the cataclysm is 143.1 ±0.6 Ma and the lower part of the Aput is 121.4 ±0.6 Ma, leaving 130 Ma near the Barem to Apt. [3] This is the timereference recorded by Fossil and is not the exact moment of origin of the subdivisions.

Common misunderstandings

One of the misunderstandings is that the first flower is a flower of a modern style. The structure of the flowers, embryos and fruits of the early sub-plants may be simple and cannot be imagined by the orchids or roses of today. [1] Another misconception is that the sudden increase in the number of Fossil records is attributable entirely to the acceleration of evolution; conditions of preservation, collection intensity and exposure to statigraphy also affect the “first-to-first” position. [2]

Relationship to other phases

The phytophytes emerged in the early and late days, becoming the subject of many land communities and creating new interactions with insects and herbivores. [1][2] The process of replacing naked plants is uneven, with different regions having different rhythms from habitat. [3] The share of phytophytophytes in tropical and temperate forests has increased in late platinum periods, but expansiontimes on different continents are not consistent. [1][3]
