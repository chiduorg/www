Molecular Traces of Early Eukaryotes

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Leather molecular relics in the rock of about 1.6 billion years ago provide clues for the study of early and complex cells. The research team linked the original gill records in it to the ancient nuclei; this evidence does not amount to a determination of the origin of the real nuclear organism as the only global age.

What is it?

Early real nuclear life refers to a complex cell spectrum with a cell nuclear and membrane system. This article deals with the record of a class of molecule Fossil: the protosterol molecule, which was kept in Rock some 1.6 billion years ago, is interpreted as the chemical relics of the early relatives of the real nuclear organisms. [1][2]

How?

Researchers extracted the original aromium and the associated aromatic aromium from sedimentary rocks in northern Australia some 1.6 billion years ago and identified molecular structures by pyrolysis against standard products. According to ANU reports, these molecules come from a group of micro-organisms known as the “preliminary cylindrical biota”, an age of at least 1.6 billion years, which may extend to about 800 million years. The study also found that such molecules were repeated in multiple basins more than a billion years ago, suggesting that records were not random. Molecular Fossil preserves only chemical biomarkers and does not preserve the cell form; biological attribution relies on a comparison of the thorium synthesis path with the current class. [1][2] It is therefore molecular evidence and not a complete direct observation of the cell. [2]

Time Coordinates and Measurement condition

Benentry takes about 1.6 billion years from today as a reference, corresponding to about 384.9×10¹⁵ seconds from Big Bang; from "1.6 billion years x 31,557,600 seconds" conversion. [1] 1.6 billion years is the approximate age of Fossilstratigraphy, and the years measured for specific samples are in millions of years; molecular records give “at least present”, not the origin cap. [2]

Common misunderstandings

The Fossil, which directly treats molecular markers as some modern real nuclear organism, is overreaded; the original gillbyota may be located in an unknown form in a genuine core. [1] Nor can 1.6 billion years be seen as the only time in which a true nuclear organism has appeared; earlier microfossil was given different limits from molecular clocks. [2]

Relationship to other phases

These records fill a lacuna in the early history of the complex cell before the Docell Nucleus Fossil after the Big Ox event. [1][2] It goes up with LUCA and goes down to Edikarabiota and Cold War diversity; but the “docellation” of “animals” is three different time thresholds. [2]
