The Great Ordovician Biodiversification Event

Otaunchi marine animals have expanded significantly in their ranks and ecolory positions, with increasing lifestyles such as reefs, benthic habitats and swimming hunting. What is it?

Otaunchi radiation is an extension of marine animals in Otaunchi. It is reflected in a fourfold increase in animal size, a simultaneous increase in lifestyles such as reefs, benthic filtering and swimming feeding, and the emergence of stratification structures similar to modern forests in the benthic community. [1] This is not the sudden birth of a new class, but rather the process of existing animals being broken down in ecologyspace.

How?

The judgement is based mainly on a combination of fossil in sea-level statigraphy: the diversity of trifles, quails, wrist-footed animals, corals and thorny animals has increased significantly in profile. The researchers place the regular numbers and ecology types of different regions in the same time frame comparison and consider differences between rock preservation and sampling density. [1][2] The reasons remain inconclusive, and changes in sea level, volcanic activity, biological interaction and species formation rates are presented, and a single interpretation is not sufficient to cover global records. About 470 Ma is the usual reference for the Middle Otause. According to the International Stratigphy Commission, version 2024/12, the base level of the lawn is 471.3 ±1.4 Ma and the bottom level of Darywell is 469.4 ± 0.9 Ma, so the round figure falls on the early part of Central Otayu. Common misunderstandings

A common misconception is to view large radiation as a replica of the cold-blooded outbreak, so that it becomes more and more for all animals at the same time. Fossil records show a sequential expansion of different groups, with inconsistent rhythms. Another misunderstanding is the complexity of increasing the ratio to directly represent the entire ecology system; preservation deviations for molluscs and microfossil would make the roster incomplete. Relationship to other phases

In the immediate aftermath of the cold martial arts era, Otaki filled more eclogy positions with the framework of the animals established by the cold martial arts; the fall of the Gwana glacier and sea level at the end of Otaki caused another mass extinction that interrupted the expansion. After [2][3], the legacy and mudslides shifted to reef reconstruction and fish abundance, and the way in which marine communities were organized changed again. [1]
