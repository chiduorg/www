Gradual Neutrino Decoupling

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The neutrino decoupling occurs during an interaction that is becoming increasingly difficult to maintain. PDF outlines the back-to-back background of about 2 MeVs; 1 second is a round time reference, not a common cut-off for all tastes.

What is it?

Neutrons exchange energy through weak interactions with plasma. Early temperature was high and responsive, with all three neutrons maintaining their motheral equilibrium; when universe swelled to about 2 MeV, it gradually became less active than the inflation rate, and the neutrons began to exit balance and spread freely. [1] Decoupling is not a separate "off switch" but a gradual exchange of odor oscillations with Electron's defunct head. [1][3]

How do you know or why?

Today's neutron background has not been directly detected. PDG states that its presence is indirectly supported by three aspects: the original lightness abundance, the heterogeneity spectrum of the microwave background and the concentration of the larger scale structure. [1] In 2019, the international cooperation team measured the statistical impact of neutrinos on density fluctuations, linking the standard thermal history to today ' s larger scale structure, using a BOSS patrol position of approximately 1.2 million galaxy. [2] This remains indirect evidence and is not the case for the capture of neutrons in each relic.

Time Coordinates and Measurement condition

Standard thermal history tends to place weak decoupling in the vicinity of Big Bang about one second, temperature around 1 MeV; PDF sums up the beginning of gradual decoupling with about 2 MeV. [1][3] The two figures are not the same: temperature is given by comparison of reaction and inflation rates, and the seconds are subject to a history of radiation-dominated expansion and effective freedom. [1][3] This article takes one second for navigation, which is a second-grade order of magnitude and does not mean that all neutrons leave the hot bath at the same time in that second. [1]

Common misunderstandings

Common misconceptions are that “decoupling” is locked in a box as a particle or that movement is stopped suddenly. Decoupling only means that collisions are not sufficient to maintain the balance, and neutrinos are still free to pass through the material and do not reheat later. [1][2] Another misunderstanding is to read one second as a precision measurement year; the actual values vary with the withdrawal of temperature, odor oscillation and electron treatment of desolation, only as a reference to the model era. [1][3]

Relationship to other phases

The decoupling took place before the original nuentron synthesis of about 180 seconds, providing the background for the latter to fix the neutron-proton comparison; the subsequent electron-exerctron destruction gave the entropy more to the photons, leaving the microtons in the remains below the photons. [1][3] Free dissemination after decoupling affects density fluctuations in complex times, so neutrino background intentry shares the same hot history as this article, but different concepts. [1][2]
