Early Ediacara Biota

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Edikara-type large fossil is well documented in some of the deep water deposits about 575 million years ago. The emergence, distribution and preservation of these organisms need to be distinguished through a statigraphy study, and the beginning of the entire Edikara cannot be taken directly as the starting point of the biota.

What is it?

Edikarabiota refers to a large, soft body, Fossil, which appeared in Edikaraki (about 635-541 million years ago) and is often preserved in leaves, discs or tube marks. [1][2] They are not simple previews of animal species, but a group of macro-organisms with unique morphology and still controversial classification positions. [2]

How?

Studies documented by the United States Geological Survey found the Edikara Fossil in deep water sediments in the Yukon, Canada, before the suram carboniisotope anomaly, some 575 million years ago. The paper also contrasts deep-water with shallow-water profiles, suggesting that such organisms may first appear in deep-water and then expand to the shelf for about 15 million years. [1] Stratigraphyentry of Brittany encyclopaedia set the Edikara Framework between 635 and 541 million years ago, stating that its Fossil was dominated by soft body marks. [2] Fossil is preserved only at a given particle size and sediment rate, and the missing layer is not equal to the fact that life never existed. These are a combination of evidence of statigraphy, antiquities and carboniisotope, not direct observations of living life. [1]

Time Coordinates and Measurement condition

Benentry took the distance of 575 million years to make early Fossil records reference, corresponding to approximately 417.3×10¹⁵ seconds from Big Bang; round age conversion. [1] 575 million years is the approximation of the Fossil layer geogenic age, not the beginning of the Edikara; some 635 million years ago, the difference was about 60 million years. [2]

Common misunderstandings

It is common confusion to use the beginning of Edikara as the starting point of the biota; large Fossil is recorded later in the year. [1][2] Another misconception is that all Eddie Karafossil is treated as an animal. Their kinship may include animal herds, fungi or other fungus that cannot be generalized. [2]

Relationship to other phases

Edikarabiota was the first widespread stage of macrolife after the snowball ice period on Earth and before the cold war was diversified. [1][2] It is approximately one billion years apart from the Docell Nucleus, indicating that “Docellization” is not the same as “large soft body biota”. [1]
