What is it?

Artificial synthesis refers to a reactor's neutron or accelerator heavy ion beam that transforms one atom nuclear into another. Common misunderstandings

Artificial synthesis is not equivalent to the non-existence of universe: plutonium has traces of natural products in uranium mines, which are extremely difficult to extract. Overweight element is not equivalent to “totally measured nature”: the ionizing, radius, electrical negatives of most elements after 109 are still not experimental, only theoretically predicted. In addition, the synthesis of an atom is not the same as “discovering an element”, which requires independent repetition and formal identification.
