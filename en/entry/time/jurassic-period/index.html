Jurassic Period

Using the 2024/12 edition of the International Stratigphy Committee ' s geological age sheet, Jurassicism lasted from 201.4 million years to 143.1 million years. It's a time of many ecology and geographic changes, not the first moment of a dinosaur.

What is it?

Jurassic is the second of the Middle Ages, after the Sanctuary, before the Platinum. It is known for its pancontinental division, climate strips and the large-scaleization of dinosaurs, and the United States Morrison Group and Fossil reservoirs such as Sorenhoven limestone, Germany, have preserved a wealth of marine and terrestrial life. [1] Marine invertebrates, naked plants and terrestrial vertebrates all changed significantly over this period. The oceans are rich in pyrestones and double-hulls, and the naked plants on land form the main vegetation, and these groups provide the mark Fossil for statigraphy. [1][2]

Jurassic ' s gerogical age frame is derived from the biological statigraphy of Sea-Astropics, wrist-foot and microform fossil, plus the year of radioactivityiisotope compared to magnetic stantigraphy. Time Coordinates and Measurement condition

Using International Stratigphy Committee version 2024/12: Jurassic from 201.4 ± 0.2 Ma to 143.1 ± 0.6 Ma. [3] Some of the teaching materials and institutional pages are still written from 201.3 to 145.0 Ma, with variations from the version of the geological page and the soundarydefinition. The time axis begins with a reference to the baseary, and the number of seconds does not mean that the baseary age is accurate to the second.

Common misunderstandings

One of the misunderstandings was to consider Jurassic as a climate and ecology-like period. In fact, the pancontinental division has resulted in a change in land and sea distribution and the climate belt, with marked differences in the biological composition of different regions. Another misconception is that the “dinosaur gold age” is understood to be the largest number of dinosaurs; Jurassic large iguanas do flourish, but the diversity of the breath is equally high. [1] There was also a misunderstanding that the Jurassic dinosaurs were all in the same category; the iguana, animal and bird buttocks had been divided into different shapes and diets. [1]

Relationship to other phases

Jurassic has been left empty since the end of the era of extinction, and dinosaurs, as well as pristine and nudist plants have expanded significantly. [2] It ended at the beginning of the era of white cyanide, where flowering plants and new dinosaurs continue to rewrite the terrestrial ecology system. [1][3]
