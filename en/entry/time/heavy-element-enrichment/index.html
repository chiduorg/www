Early Chemical Enrichment

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Star evolution and the material cycle will gradually change the composition of the element of the universe gas. ALMA detected oxygen in early GalaxyJADES-GS-z14-0, providing evidence of chemical enrichment in the post-Big Bang era of about 300 million years; it was not just an event.

What is it?

Astronomy calls helium-weighted element “metals”, including carbon, oxygen, iron, etc. [3]. They are generated mainly from fusion within Stars and return to the interstellar medium [3] in supernova or Star winds. As a result, the universe gas has gradually been enriched, followed by the formation of star and planets. The concentration of heavy element is not an event, but the result of the accumulation of many Star generations.

How?

ALMA detected the oxidation line of JADES-GS-z14-0 in two teams; the phototime of the galaxy was approximately 13.4 billion years, corresponding to universe under 300 million years of age [1][2]. The presence of oxygen indicates that it has evolved through star and spilled heavy element into the gas instead of remaining the original ingredient. The spectra also shows that its heavy element content is about 10 times [2] expected. It's a spectrograph, not against a single Star's observation.

Time Coordinates and Measurement condition

This article takes about 300 million years as the era of observation, from JADES-GS-z14-0 for red migration and lighttime transfer[1][2]. This is an era of “enrichment”, not the moment when the first Star dies or first oxygen generation takes place; it continues between the Star generations. observation only gives the galaxy the state of the era and cannot directly push the whole unverse. The chemical maturity rates of different galaxy can vary significantly.

Common misunderstandings

One of the misunderstandings is that the concentration of element is considered a global event; the chemical evolution of different Galaxy and different regions is not synchronized. Another misconception is that life is directly demonstrated by the oxygen spectrum; it only indicates chemical maturity. Nor can the "explosive oxygen detection" be considered as the first generation of oxygen[2] by universe. In addition, the abundance of element is calculated using spectrolines and models and is not directly weighed. Carbon, iron, etc. are added to oxygen, which is produced by different nucleination pathways.

Relationship to other phases

The first generation of stars and galaxy is connected to the concentration of element, forming together with the row galaxy[3]. The first generation of Star manufactures the first metals, and galaxy combines them with gas flows and mixes them. It is older than the solar system, and it also prepares raw materials for rock planets and elements for life. Compared to midday, the focus here is on chemical evolution rather than total star formation. It shows that earlier galaxy matured faster than some models expected.
