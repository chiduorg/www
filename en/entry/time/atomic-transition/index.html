Time / Durations

Atomic Transition

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Atom leap means Electron is converted between levels of energy; the average duration of the active spontaneous radiation is the lifetime of the spontaneous radiation. The typical lifetime of the electrophages allowed to leapfrog is about 10⁻⁹ to 10⁻⁸ seconds.

What is it?

Atom leaps mean Electron transitions between levels of energy; when atoms move from activation to lower energy and emit photons, the process has an average continuitytime, known as spontaneous radiation lifetime. The typical lifetime of the electrophages allowed to leapfrog is about 10⁻⁹ to 10⁻⁸ seconds. [1] It describes “the duration of the active state”, not “when the atom is inspired”.

How?

Life expectancy is determined by the Einstein A coefficient: the greater the chances of leaping, the shorter the life expectancy. The NIST atom spectra database system compiles leap-frogging probabilities and energy-level data for all spectral lines, which are experimentally derived from natural width of the measurement spectra or fluorescence resolution. [2] Britannica summarizes the average time of spontaneous radiation as10⁻⁹ to 10⁻⁸ seconds and emphasizes that it is a probabilistic process rather than a fixed moment. [1] Values given in different spectrums and at different levels of power vary by more than one order of scale.

Value and Measurement condition

10⁻⁹ to seconds apply to common electrophages that allow leaps; the ban can last for seconds or even longer, for example, in some sub-stable situations. [1][2] Life is also affected by collisions, radiation fields and temperature, with measurement in thin gases coming closer to the inner limit. Citiation should indicate which spectrum and under what conditions, and should not consider “10⁻⁸ seconds” as a single time frame for all tom jumps. [2]

How long does this last, not when?

This is a stimulating mean time, a continuous time; a single atom can only be described by a probability distribution and cannot predict an absolute moment.

Common misunderstandings

One of the misunderstandings is that life expectancy is understood to be a “point to point”. Real decay follows the index distribution, with average life expectancy being only a statistical parameter. [1] There is also a misunderstanding about separating the transient lifetime from the spectral width: the natural width is inversely related to the lifespan, which is also a way of measuring lifetime. [2]

Relationship with other scale

10⁻⁸ seconds are about tens of millions of times shorter than a heartbeat, 100,000 times shorter than a protein folding (approximately 10⁻³ seconds) and hundreds of millions more than a strong interactiontime (approximately 10⁻²³ seconds). The atom leap between macrotime and nuclear processes is the grass-roots clock that understands how “light produces”. [1][2]
