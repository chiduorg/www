What is it?

The last generation of Stars points to the future point at which the formation of Stars in the UNiverse has largely stopped, and the last group of low-mass red dwarfs have depleted fuel and left a secret trail. Model, which often evolves over time, is placed in 10^14 after about 3.16×10^21seconds (from Big Bang). [1][2] It is not the exact date of death of a Star, but the end of the Star era.

How?

Star glows on core hydrogen fusion, and the smaller the mass, the longer it lasts. Adams and Laughtlin summed up that the supply of interstellar gases had been depleted about 1 to 10 trillion years later, and that the formation ofstars could only continue at very low rates of impact through brown dwarfs. [1] The course by Ohio State designated the Star era as approximately 10^6to 10^14 and noted that the red dwarf after10^14had burned out and that the remaining material had been locked into black dwarfs, cold neutrons and black holes. [2] These findings are derived from the Star evolution calculations and the gas consumption of models, rather than future observation.

Common misunderstandings

The last generation of Stars is not the equivalent of “all Stars going out together”. Mass's bigstar is long dead, and the red dwarf can burn for hundreds of millions of years; the last remaining Star's color is red and bright. [1][2] Another misunderstanding is that there is nothing left of universe after Star's disappearance; white dwarfs, black dwarfs, neutrons and black holes will remain in place for a long time, except that there are no new sources of fusion light. Nor can “star formation stop” be considered the same moment as “the last starlight disappears”.

Relationship with other scale

The last generation of stars is the peak of star formation and the evolution of galaxy, the era of simplicity, the age of the black hole and the health path. [1][2] It's much later than the Sun's Red Star phase (about 5 billion years later) and far before the evaporation of the black hole (about 10^100 years later). It marks the end of the fusion-led universe, not the end of the universe itself.
