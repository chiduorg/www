The Era of Early Dinosaurs

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Dinosaurs were found in the middle of the triarchy some 245 million years ago, and the late triad record some 230 million years ago contained a wide range of identifiable early members. Fossil was not the origin of the branch, and the location of early dinosaurs would still be adjusted to new materials.

What is it?

Dinosauria is the Dinosauria branch, where the first approximately 245 Ma appeared in the middle of the trimester and then took a long-term position on land. [1] The late triad of about 230 Ma already contains a variety of identifiable early dinosaurs, which are small in size and coexist with other reptiles. [1][2] Early dinosaurs were only part of the fauna at the time and did not occupy all the environment immediately upon arrival. It was only in the early years of Jurassic that dinosaurs significantly increased their body size and spread after the end of the tripolar era. [2]

How?

The determination of dependence on bone characteristics: hip structure, limb joints and skull details separate dinosaurs from other reptiles. [1] Fossil is produced from tri-circle subsurface red layer and lake sedimentation, and the researchers, in conjunction with statigraphy comparison and system development analysis, determine the sequence of different specimens. [2] Early dinosaur Fossil is scarce, specimen integrity is limited and classification locations are adjusted with new discoveries. [1]

Time Coordinates and Measurement condition

About 230 Ma is in the late Triple Kani period. According to the International Stratigphy Commission, version 2024/12, the Kani period ranges from 237 to 227.3 Ma. [3] This coordinates are the stage of the emergence of the early dinosaur Fossil and are not the origin of the dinosaur branch; the earlier Fossil or the unsaved branch may push the actual division even earlier.

Common misunderstandings

One of the misunderstandings is to think of dinosaurs as “the rule of Earth as soon as it emerges”. They are only part of the terrestrial fauna at the time of the third chronology and will not expand significantly until the end of the third chronology. [2] Another misconception is that all mid-generation reptiles are called dinosaurs; Wing Dragons, Snakes and Fish Dragons are different branches. [1] There is also a misconception that dinosaurs are opposed to birds; Fossil evidence suggests that birds are embedded in animal feet. [1]

Relationship to other phases

Dinosaurs entered the prime of Jurassic after the end of the Trigena and continue to diversify in the cataclysm. [2][3] Birds have evolved from an animal foot dinosaur, so the extinction of the non-bird dinosaur K-Pg has not ended the entire dinosaur branch. [1] Dinosaurs continue to divide between Jurassic and Platinum, with only birds eventually crossing the K-Pg boundary. [1][3]

The error book of this value.

The main evidence of the extinction of non-bird dinosaurs at the end of the white circa some 66 million years ago was the combination of the Hiksuluber impact and the degenerative volcanic activity. 66 million years is round value, with different statigraphy and isotope measuring years giving about 66.04 million years of age, and roror in tens of thousands of years. Writing the term “dimonocide” as a single moment would ignore the disappearance of different groups and areas.

Another scale to look at it.

Sixty-six million years is about 100 times the history of human civilization and about 1.4 per cent of the Earth. Dinosaurs rule the land for about 160 million years, more than 20 times the history of mankind. If the Earth's history is turned into a day, the dinosaurs appear in the last 20 minutes and disappear in the last few seconds; the human text is only the last few seconds.
