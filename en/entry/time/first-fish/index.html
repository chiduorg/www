Early Cambrian Vertebrates

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

About 518 million years ago, Mongjiang Fossil preserved one of the earliest known vertebrates. They have head, thorium fractures and ectoplasms, but they have no acne, nor do they have a complete hard skeleton for modern fish; Fossil is not the beginning of the whole branch.

What is it?

This is about the fish vertebrates in the early era of the cold war, which included Myllokunmingia and Haikouichthys. They already have basic structures for vertebrates, such as head, thorium fractures and muscular muscles, but they do not have an argon or a solid skeleton as complete as modern fish. [1] These physical characteristics place them near the base of the vertebrates, rather than in an active group of modern fish.

How?

Fossil is kept in the fine granite of Yunnan ' s Luongjiang, and the mollusc parts leave marks in the form of carbon thin membranes. A study in 2026 identified pine and pine omelet with mellokunmingiid in black between the sides, and interpreted the ovary structure as crystals, thereby suggesting that they had four imageable camera eyes. [1] This is a comparative anatomy interpretation of the preservation structure and is not a direct observation of living behaviour.

Time Coordinates and Measurement condition

The original paper Summary gave about 518 Ma. [1] According to the International Stratigphy Commission, version 2024/12, the lower limit of the third edition of the cold war was approximately 521 Ma, so 518 Ma was in the early stages of the cold war. [3] This figure is derived from the age frame containing Fossilstratigraphy and is not equal to the origin of the entire vertebrate family; the earlier members would not have appeared in the record if they had not kept it.

Common misunderstandings

One of the misunderstandings is that one of the members of Tsurujiang is a direct ancestor of all fish stocks. It is only one of the earliest known vertebrates forssil, and the specific spectral position will be adjusted with new materials. [1] Another misconception is to read “the earliest Fossil” as “the day of origin”; the emergence of Fossil is limited by the sedimentary environment and the conditions for preservation, and the true division should take place earlier. [1][2]

Relationship to other phases

The Sanctuary herds were part of the cold-blooded radiation when several species, including vertebrates, were quickly established. [2] [3] Since then, the absence of aphids has continued to expand in Otukia to Shiraiki, with the rise of aphids; the mudbasin commonly known as the “fish age” has evolved from a predatory fin. [2]
