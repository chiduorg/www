What's the value?

The value of this article is 3.6 s, obtained from a base reading of "Protein Collapse" 0.001 s multiplied by 3600. The baseline entry amount is a typical time of a small uni-domain refolding protein that takes 1 millisecond to represent the milliseconds; conversion does not change the physical object, it changes the scale that we place on. [1]

How's that?

The calculation is subject to gradual review: 0.001 s x 3600 = 3.6 s. The in vitro folding of small uni-domain proteins is usually between milliseconds and seconds, often taking a representative value of 1 millisecond, i.e. 10⁻³. [1][3]
