Early Tetrapod Evolution

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Fossil, about 365 million years ago, recorded a shift in the fin to the toe-toe. The appearance of toes, the absence of bodies from water and the movement of land were not three things that had been done on the same day; the earlier division of the branches still required more Fossil testing.

What is it?

Four-footed animals are vertebrates with limbs and toes and are evolved by a finfish. The bones of the early members have appeared to the fingertips, but the body still retains a number of fish characteristics and may not necessarily be in the same shape as the later terrestrial animals. [1][2] There are thus two different things related to the “spring of legs” and “to land”.

How?

The evidence comes from the bones Fossil and the comparative autopsy in Stratigraphy in the late mud. Transitional Fossil, such as the Titalik fish, shows that the inside of the fin already has some sort of organization of later limbs; the late four-foot animal Fossil retains a clear toe. [2] Researchers place skeletal characteristics in the system development analysis and use the statigraphygeological formation branchtimetime. Such inferences rely on the integrity of the model and the specimen and cannot be considered a direct observed record of conduct. [2]

Time Coordinates and Measurement condition

About 365 Ma is a common reference for the early four-foot animal Fossil. According to the International Stratigphy Commission, version 2024/12, this figure fell between 372.15 ± 0.46 and 358.86 ± 0.19 Ma at the end of the late mud. [3] The division of the times may be earlier and the results given by the molecular clock and Fossilcalibration are not fully consistent, so the coordinates indicate only the stage at which Fossil is known to appear.

Common misunderstandings

One of the misconceptions is that the Titalik fish are the earliest four-footed animals. It is closer to the transition patterns between fish and tetrapods, and the members with the real toes appear in the late statigraphy. Another misconception is that limbs are used to walk on land as soon as they appear; researchers do not directly equate the presence of toes to life off water. [1][2]

Relationship to other phases

Four-footed animals are divided from the mosaics of fish fauna, then diversified into membrane fauna in the mosaic forests of the quarries. [1][3] Their landings altered the food web of the terrestrial ecology system and also provided the physical basis for later terrestrial life of dinosaurs and mammals. [2]
