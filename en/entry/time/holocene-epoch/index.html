Holocene Epoch

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Holocene was the second of the fourth century, starting some 11.7 thousand years ago and continuing to this day. It is the current ice period. It is also a time framework for agriculture, villages, words and industrial civilization.

What is it?

Holocene was the second century of the fourth century, which began some 11.7 thousand years ago and continues to this day, and is currently in the middle of an ice period. With the retreat of glaciers, vegetation and animals have regained control of areas previously affected by the ice sheet; humans have moved from hunting and gathering to farming and livestock farming, with villages, towns, scripts and countries emerging, and industrialization and global trade have pushed human activities to new dimensions. [2][3] This coordinates is the point of departure of the world, not the whole of Holocene.

How?

Holocene ' s géological age framework relies on ice cores, lake marsh deposits, tree vessels, corals and archaeological sites. Radioactivity carbon year, volcanic ash layer, together with historical records, provide time markers; sapling and plant debris revegetation, oxygeniisotope and ice core bubble recordclimate. In 2018, the International Stratigphy Commission formally divided Holocene into Greenland, North Greenland and Meghalaya, marked by the boundaries of about 11.7 thousand years, 8,300 years and 4,200 years, respectively. [1][2]

Time Coordinates and Measurement condition

The International Stratigphy Commission, version 2024/12, marked the floor of Holocene, which is the base of Greenland, as 0.0117 million years ago, and about 117,000 years ago. [1] Conversion to about 13,797 million years since Big Bang. This is the beginning of the world, not the whole Holocene. Holocene had been covering very different processes, such as agricultural origin, invention and the industrial revolution, for more than 10,000 years; condensing the entire history into a “human age” label would mask the enormous changes. Seconds are used only for sorting.

Common misunderstandings

One of the common misconceptions is that Holocene remains stable as a climate. Early increased monsoons in Holocene, wetter Sahara, warmer parts of the centre, followed by new ice-style fluctuations with significant regional variations. Another misconception is to equate “human impacts” with Holocene's global synchronization from the outset; significant land conversion, deforestation and species migration are uneven in terms of size and intensity. [3]

Relationship to other phases

Holocene inherited the ice age of the newer age and the extinction of large animals, which continues to this day. Its starting point coincides with the end point of the renewal, which is a different name at the same level; “Anthropocene” has been proposed in recent years as a new world, but has not yet become a formal statigraphy unit and discussions continue. [1][2]
