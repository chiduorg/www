ABOUT / ENGLISH EDITION

Readable now,
reviewed next.

This English edition is an MVP: 60 features and 450 entries translated with Argos Translate, protected notation, a 110-term working glossary and deterministic post-processing.

What is not claimed

No native English editor has reviewed this text. Pages are labelled “machine-assisted draft · native review pending”; they are not presented as externally reviewed or publication-ready prose.

What was checked automatically

Every page is checked for Chinese-character leakage, broken citation links, missing sources and hreflang/canonical consistency. Math and scientific notation are protected before translation and restored afterwards, so values such as 4.354×10¹⁷ s are not converted into “1017”.

Source hierarchy

The Chinese entry is the reviewed source. Its source list is carried into the English page. Where the machine draft is ambiguous, the Chinese entry and its cited source prevail.

Next review pass

Native review should focus on terminology, sentence rhythm, idiom and claims that are culturally or historically sensitive. The working glossary is in content/i18n/en/glossary.json; corrections should update the glossary and the page, not just one sentence.
