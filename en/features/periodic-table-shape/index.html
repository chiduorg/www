Why the periodic table looks like this

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The periodic table is not a memory table, but a space projection of the capacity of the Electron shell. Mendeleev left a space, Moselle used to sequence the Quantum mechanics to explain the length of the cycle.

The birth of an experience sheet

In 1869, Mendeleev was ranked according to the atomic weight and chemical properties and found a cyclical repetition of nature. He left a space and predicted the nature of unknown elements, such as gills, which were later discovered and largely established. [1] This table was an empirical pattern at the time and no quantum interpretation was available.

Atomic number sequence

In 1913, Marcelle used an X-ray spectrum to prove that the positive charge of element was the basis for ranking, not atomic weight. After Z, the original positions “atomic weight upside down” are naturally in place. [2] This step changes element from a chemical classification to atom of the same number of protons.

Shell Capacity

Quantum mechanics gives s, p, d, f sublevel capacity: 2, 6, 10, 14. Fill it in energy order, with a cycle length of 2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, 32. [3] Only 1s for the first cycle, 2s/2p and 3s/3p for the second and third cycles, and 3d for the fourth cycle.

Blocks and Locations

The s area is the 1st, 2nd and Helium, the p area is the 13th-18th, the d area is the transitional metal, and the f area is the gills and gills. The helium, which is located in the 18th family but belongs to the s sector, is a typical example of “location subject to electron structures”. [1][3] The periodic table is therefore not a drawing by nature, but a structure chart in the order of filling.

Overweight eliment

The seventh cycle to Z = 118, with all isotope having radioactivity; the heavier element may extend to 8s, but it is not known how long it can be synthesized. [1][2] Stable Island is merely a theoretical projection that has not yet been confirmed. The periodic table may be expanded, but “identified” is separated from “possible location”.

Common misunderstandings

The common misconception is that the cyclical table is complete. It includes confirmed or named element, which may continue structurally; however, new element synthesis is limited by nuclear stability and experimental capabilities. [2] Another misunderstanding is that “the homologue is identical in nature”; the homogeneity is the approximation of the valence electron structure, with a marked difference between cycles and blocks.

What's the length of the cycle? 2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, 32.

The length of the cycle is determined by the capacity of the Electron shell: s for 2,p for 6,d for 10,f for 14. For the first cycle, only 1s, so 2; for the second and third cycles ns, np, so 8; for the fourth and fifth cycles (n-1)d, so 18; for the sixth and seventh cycles (n-2)f, so 32. [1] These figures are not memory devices, but a cumulative sub-layer capacity.

Relative effects and gold, mercury

1s electron velocity at heavy element is near light speed, mass increases, orbit shrinks, and 6s orbits are down. 5d →6s of gold leaps to absorb blue light and is therefore golden yellow; 6s of mercury in closed crust and relativism shrinks the metal key and the melting point is low to constant temperature. [2] The `unusuals' of the periodic tables are often derived from relativism rather than misdrawing the tables.

What do you call overweight element?

New element is subject to double-testing, decay chain confirmation and IUPAC audit to obtain a name and symbol. The synthesis of oganesson (Z=118) is very small atom, half-life in milliseconds; a heavier element may have landed near a stable island but has not yet been confirmed. [3] Periodic tables can be drawn to 8s, but "can paint" does not mean "can synthesize".

Three keys to read the watch.

Read an element, first the cycle (main shell layer), family (valence electron), blocks (last filled sublayer); then the atomic number and isotope; and lastly, its abnormalities in the cycle, such as chromium, copper rags, the contraction and relativity effects. [1][3] The table then changes from a memory map to a structural reasoning tool.

Ethnic and chemical properties

Element of the same species has a similar valence electron configuration, so the response pattern is similar: alkali metal is vulnerable to a Electron, halogen is easy to acquire an Electron, and rare gas casings are closed. [1] However, the term “same” is not identical; the upper and lower radius increases and the metal is increased, with the difference between the second and sixth cycles being particularly marked.

Why did you leave the gills alone?

F Area element is too broad if you press tomic number; it is placed separately for layout purposes and does not mean that they are not part of the periodic table. [2] The contraction of the thorium makes the thorium and thorium and tantalum very close and makes separation difficult.

Table

The periodic tables organize the nature of element and predictions that are known, but do not explain molecular structure, reaction rate and material performance in isolation; these require quantum chemistry, termodynamics and kinetics. [3] The table is an index, not a complete theory.

A common reverse example.

The periodic table is not a simple result of " Electron type by grid ". The actual layout of chromium, copper and so forth deviates from the desired order, with relativity amendments; the table location is determined jointly with the Electron configuration, but the configuration itself requires quantum mechanics.
