Rest mass: why is it invariant?

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Quietmass is the mass of the object in its own static reference system and is also a non-variant of the four-dimensional kinetic square. Sport increases energy, but doesn't keep rest mass.

Start with 4D kinetics.

Relativity synthesizes energy and momentum into four dimensions. Quiet mass m0 meets m02c4 = E2 − p2c2; this combination is the same in all inertia, so rest mass is not a variable. [1] E changes with p, but their combination remains unchanged.

Why don't you say, "Run harder."

The early materials call gamma-m0 "relativitymass" to make high-speed objects look heavier. Modern particle physics uses more frequently the expression “rest mass, energy and momentum increase” because “relativity mass” combines different concepts. [2] The rest mass of particles in the accelerator is constant and the energy is increased.

Special case of photons

Photons are zero, E = pc, so no rest mass has energy and momentum. It's not a "micromass", it's a zeromass particle; E/c2 is an equivalent mass that cannot be replaced by a rest mass formula. [1][3] The neutrino has a very small rest mass, but not a zero, and can thus occupy a very light position on the mass axis.

Combining with mass losses

Atom's nuclear rest mass is smaller than the sum of proton and neutron rest mass that makes it, and the difference corresponds to the combined power. When nuclear fission and fusion release energy, the rest mass of the product decreases. [3] This is not “mass gone”, but rest mass is transformed into other forms of energy and momentum.

Common misunderstandings

A common misconception is that “quiet mass increases with speed”. Silent mass is not a variable, and the speed increases in total energy and momentum. [1][2] Another misperception is that “Zero Quietmass” is considered to be “no energy”; photon just means that the two are not the same.

4D momentum

Relativity gives a four-dimensional vector of energy and kinetics with a constant length of rest mass: m2c4 = E2 - p2c2. [1] Georest mass is a nonvariant of reference, with different observers calculating different levels of energy and momentum, but combining the same m.

Why isn't it still?

Changing the system changes the energy and momentum, but the relationship remains unchanged; photons are static at zero, but there is energy and momentum. [2] "Geostationarymass" is not "mass in static time" but a non-variant of 4D kinetics.

Neutron mass

Neutrons were considered to be mass-free, but the oscillation experiment showed a mixture of three odours, requiring a non-zero mass difference; absolute massscale was still pending. [3] This is the direct application of the rest mass concept in particle physics.

Why is the photons still zero?

Photons move at light speed, with no static reference system; their energy and kinetic content E=pc is constant and m=0. [1] Photons are still energy-absorbed by matter.

No change mass with systemmass

A system of multiple particles whose constant mass includes both kinetic and dynamic contributions does not mean that the sum of the particles is static. [2] Thus, the "systemic weighting" can come from energy rather than from new particles.

How do you do that?

Particle physics uses constant lengths of 4-dimensional kinetics to produce mass and error for particles like Electron and Proton. neutrino oscillation indicates that mass is not zero, but absolute scale is still pending. [4] [5]

No change mass is not as simple as a “rest mass”: it is a irrelevant combination of reference, and no change mass of systems includes internal kinetic and dynamic energy. TreatE/c² directly as a rest mass that ignores the kinetic item.
