What is mass? Four ideas behind one scale

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Mass is not "how much." It points to inertia, gravity, rest mass and energy; the same scales are not in the same amount in different contexts.

Start with an operation

In the laboratory, the most direct operation of mass is resistance to acceleration: to give the object a known force to measure its acceleration, mass is the ratio of force to acceleration. This definition corresponds to inertia mass, in kg. [1] It is not philosophy, but can be repeated in scales, vibration strings, mass spectrometers or collision experiments.

Inertial mass and gravity mass

Inertial mass appears in F = ma, describing the ease with which objects change their motion; gravity mass appears in F = G m1m2/r2, describing the strength of the object to generate and feel gravity. Different from the two, they are equal at very high accuracy, which is the basis for the experiments on the principle of equivalence. [2] A free fall is not a coincidence, but a proportional result of the two masses.

Quiet mass versus “relativity”

Quietmass is mass in the body's own static reference system and is a non-variant that does not change with the reference system. The increase in energy and momentum during high-speed movement, but the rest mass, and the weighting of “relativity” as running, is an outdated and misleading expression. [3] Photons rest mass is zero, but there is energy and momentum, so E/c2 cannot be called rest mass directly.

Mass is not synonymous with weight.

The weight is the gravity of the object in a given gravity field, in Newton; mass is the inertial/gravity attribute of the object, in kg. The same person weighs about one sixth of the Earth on Moon, and mass remains unchanged. [1][2] When the scale indicates “kg”, the actual mass is gravity or gravity-related effects, which are then assigned to mass.

When should we be clear on the matter?

When speaking of “mass”, at least make a distinction: is this a rest mass or a total body mass? Does it contain dark matter or just ordinary matter? Is it wet weight, dry weight or something? [1] [3] This station mass axis places particle rest mass, macro object mass and object mass on the same logarithmic axis, but each article is marked as mass convention; the word “mass” without reference to medical convention is simply a convenient word.

Mass is not weight.

The weight is the gravity of the object in a given gravity field, which varies from place to place; mass is the common source of inertia and gravity and does not vary from place to place in classical mechanics. [1] In Moon, the weight is approximately 1/6 of the Earth, but the mass is the same; if the scale is based on the gravity of the Earth, the reading changes and the mass does not.

Three uses of mass

The day-to-day term “mass” may mean the size, inertial size or gravity charge of the object. Classical mechanics treat them as the same amount; particle physics does not change mass; energy density is taught in unverse. [2] Different contexts and different units.

Higgs isn't all mass.

The Higgs mechanism gives the basic particle mass, but most of the mass from Proton, Neutron comes from the combined energy of quark and glue, the QCD energy. [3] So “Higgs gives everything to mass” is oversimplification.

A verifiable example

The same Electron scale is used to refer to the same object on the ground and in the elevator: the readings are too large when the elevator accelerates up and drops at a small rate, but the inertia of the object remains unchanged. [1] This means that the scales are read by force, not mass; mass is tested by comparison of scales or inertia.

Math in relativity

In relativism, the “relativity mass” of individual particles increases with speed, but modern physics is more common at moss. [2] It is misleading to say that “mass increases with speed”; no change mass is the length of the four-dimensional kinematic amount, and reference is irrelevant. [3]

Mass's development is still expanding.

From Newton's inertia mass to the Higgs mechanism, the QCD combination, mass is not a single source; Proton and Neutron are mostly from powerful interactive energy, not from the Higgs mechanism alone. [4] [5]

So “mass what” does not have a short answer to cover all scale. Particle physics does not change mass, chemical Mormass, astrophysics total mass and light; each definition corresponds to a set of measurement, not a philosophical slogan.

Mass / Planets & small bodies

Earth

The Earth's total mass is approximately 5.972×10²⁴ kg, which contains the atmosphere and oceans. Values are combined by satellite orbit tracking and gravitational field model, relative to uncertainty approximately 1×10⁻³, and are mainly limited by the accuracy of the gravitational constant G.

Mass / Stars & compact objects

Sun

1.989×10³⁰ kg, or 1 solar mass, of the Sun's total mass, is often used as a conversion unit for the entire mass ladder. The values are proposed by the International Astronomical Union for nominal solarmass parameters GM☉ divided by the gravitational constant G conversion, relative to uncertainty approximately 1×10⁻⁴.
