Centre of mass: why binaries orbit an empty point

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Quality is the weighted average position of mass distribution. When there is no external force, the system moves in a straight line at the same pace; the two stars, the Earth's moon and the solar system all move around the same mass.

Definition

The quality is the weighted average of the system's mass dollar positions: r cm = Σmiri / Σmi. [1] It is not a geometry centre, nor is it necessarily within the object; a curving or donut heart may be in the cavity.

Two stars and a moon.

When the twostars rotate around each other, the common mass is close to the larger side of the mass; if the mass is very different, it may be located inside the big star. The Moon's common core is inside the Earth, about 4,700 kilometres from it, so Moon does not turn around the center of the Earth, but around the common core. [2]

Why does it matter?

When there is no external force, the total power of the system is constant, and the physicos keep moving at even speed straight lines. Dismantling the system into internal and physical motion can simplify problems of collision, rocket, twin-star and line galaxy. [1][3] The physico-reference system is one of the most commonly used frameworks for particle physics and physical mechanics.

Heart and gravity

In the gravitational field, the mass overlaps with the gravity; in the scale, where the gravitational field changes with location, the two may differ slightly. [3] Engineering often uses “centres” for stability analysis and “mass” for kinetics; the two are not interchangeable in all scenarios.

Common misunderstandings

The common misconception is that “the mind must be in the thickest part of the object”. It depends on mass distribution, not shape thickness. [1] Another misunderstanding is that “two stars turn around the big mass of mass”; they turn around the common mass, just closer to the stars.

Quality

In the mass system, the total motion of the system is zero, and collisions are easier to calculate than decay; the laboratory system is different from the energy distribution of the mass system. [2] Quality is not a “system centre”, but rather a mass weighted average position.

Why not a geometry center?

When the mass is unevenly distributed, the mass is biased towards the larger part of the mass; the mass of a flat disk is at the centre of the circle, and the mass of a gravity is not at the centre of geometry. [3] Hearts may be located outside the object, such as those in the circle.

Where's the heart of the circle?

The cylindrical heart of the flat circle is around the centre, although there is no material there; the cylindrical is the average of mass weighted position, which can be located outside the object. [1] This is different from the Geometric Center.

Double Star Massmeasurement

The two-star orbit cycle and half-long axis satisfy Kepler's third law; when the angle is known, the total mass can be compared to mass. [2] Quality is the key to understanding dual-star dynamics.

Hearts and orbits

Planets do not revolve around the centre of the Sun, but around the heart of the solar system. Jupiter's mass is about one thousandth of the Sun, and the Sun is also shaped by Jupiter's surrounding heart; it can be used to detect extraterrestrial planets. [4]

In a multibody system, the mass coordinates are the acceleration point in the inertial system; the conversion of the two body problems into "mass velocity + relative motion" can greatly simplify orbital calculations. [5] Quality is not a geometry centre, but rather a powered source of mass.

Quality and Engineering

The weight of bridges, aircraft and cars is essentially a physical repositioning. Static stability is better when the mass is lower and closer to the supporting surface; however, kinetics also looks at the point of rotation of inertia and external forces. [1][4]

The project does not give only one “centre” number, but rather the mass coordinates and inertia. Treating the body as a point would simplify the flat motion; to describe rotation, it would also be necessary to know how mass is distributed. [5]

Mass / Planets & small bodies

Earth

The Earth's total mass is approximately 5.972×10²⁴ kg, which contains the atmosphere and oceans. Values are combined by satellite orbit tracking and gravitational field model, relative to uncertainty approximately 1×10⁻³, and are mainly limited by the accuracy of the gravitational constant G.

Mass / Planets & small bodies

Moon

Moon is the only natural satellite of the Earth, with a total mass of about 7.346×10²² kg, or about one-one-first point of the Earth. The laser moon follows the Moon orbiter and gives about 0.01 per cent relative to uncertainty, which is one of the most accurate measurements in the body mass.

Mass / Stars & compact objects

Sun

1.989×10³⁰ kg, or 1 solar mass, of the Sun's total mass, is often used as a conversion unit for the entire mass ladder. The values are proposed by the International Astronomical Union for nominal solarmass parameters GM☉ divided by the gravitational constant G conversion, relative to uncertainty approximately 1×10⁻⁴.
