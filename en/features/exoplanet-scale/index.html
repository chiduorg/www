The size and mass of exoplanets: how hard it is to see a distant world

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Ling stars give radius and view speed gives mass, both of which are systematic deviations. Density can be discussed only if it is accompanied by a radius and mass; only density can begin to guess whether it is a rock, a water world or a gas giant planet.

The first and the first.

In 1995, 51 Pegasi b was discovery: a gas giant planet with a mass of approximately8.7×10²⁶ kg, with an orbital cycle of 4.2 days, nearer to Star to form an instinct of the planet at that time. [1] It's got mass lower limit, which is not precisely mass; the visual velocity can only detect the swing of Star along the line of view.

In 1999, HD 209458b became the first extraterrestrial planet to reach Lingstar. The cyclical decrease in the brightness of the star gives the ratio of the planetary radius to the Star radius directly. [2] This planet has a mass of approximately1.39×10²⁷ kg, with a radius of slightly more than Jupiter, which is a model of “hot Jupiter”.

The radius's from Lingering.

δ is approximately equal to (Rₚ/Rₛ)². If the Star radius is known, the planetary radius can be resolved. The problem is that the star radius itself also has the distance of the error:star, the effective temperature, metal abundance and model to determine its size. [2] [3]

Orbital plane must be almost parallel to sight. Most systems do not overfly stars, so they naturally tend to be short-lived, large-range, near-star planets. The distribution of the direct number of “legit planets” cannot be considered as the true distribution of planets in universe. [3]

Mass from visual speed.

Star is pulled by planetary gravity and cyclical doppler shifts occur in spectral lines. It is associated with planets mass, orbital inclinations and stellar mass; when the inclinations are not known, onlyMsin i, or mass lower limit, is available. [1][3]

Density is the first thick sift to distinguish the “rock” from the “gas” of the “water world”, but it is not the only proof; the same density can be spelled out by different internal structures. [1]

Super Earth trouble.

The mass of GJ 1214b is approximately3.9×10²⁵ kg and the radius is approximately 2.7 times the Earth radius. This combination falls on the baseary of "super Earth" and "minus Neptune": it may be a thick water vapour or helium, but it cannot be separated from the radius by mass alone. [1][4]

To distinguish, atmospheric spectra, cloud nature and internal model are required. The Ling spectra can give clues to the atmospheric composition, but the signals are only a million-thousand order of magnitude, observationtime, instrument systems Error and Star activity. [5] So Water World is often a model conclusion, not a photograph.

How hard it is to see a distant world.

Direct imaging is more difficult: the planets are a few times darker of magnitude than Star. Self-adaptation optical, coronal and infrared detection can crush the star light, but it still applies mainly to young, hot and distant planets. [5]

That is why the “scale” of the outer planets always appears in pairs: radius, mass, density, orbit, tymperature, each with the threshold of observation. The word "Earth Size" only answered the radius, not whether it was Earth. [3] [4]

When reading this type of data, see if it gives both radius and mass; then whether the sample is affected by the impact of alterrestrial or visual velocity selection; and finally whether the author has written “model allowed” as “observation has arrived”. These three steps are more useful than remembering a planet's name. [1][3]

Sample deviation with "Earth 2.0"

The Algorithm method is the easiest to find “big, hot, near” planets: large radiuss, strong shielding signals; short cycles, multiple repetitions; near Star, high temperature. The Earth's size, long-term cycle, and the temperature's modest planets, with small and slow signals, often take years to confirm. [3]

There is also a bias in the vision speed approach: the big mass planet pulls Star more clearly and the cyclicality of short-cycle planets is easier to isolate from noise. Both methods are strong but not “universe census”. [1][4]

The term “Earth 2.0” usually means only that “the radius is near the Earth and is located in the so-called habitable zone”, and does not mean that the atmosphere, magnetic fields, plates and oceans have arrived. The habitable belt is only an orbital range that may exist in liquid water and is not proof of life. [3]

A more conservative formulation is the listing of four numbers: radius, mass lower limit, orbital cycle, balancing temperature; and an indication of each number of observation methods and error. Without these four, "like Earth" is just an adjective. [1][4]

What's after density?

Density can only divide planets into a few broad categories and cannot directly give the atmospheric composition. To determine whether a planet has a thick atmosphere, it requires a hyperplanet spectra, phase curve or direct imaging; these observation signals are smaller and more susceptible to star activity. [4] [5]

So “scale” is not a number of extraterrestrial planets, but a series of mutually binding amounts. For every additional observation, model space is smaller; for every less observation, the adjective in the title should be more conservative. [1][3]
