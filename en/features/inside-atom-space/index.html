Inside the atom: is it really 99.999% empty?

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The atom core is only a fraction of the atomscale, but the electron cloud is not “empty”, but a probability distribution. The three things that make toom a small solar system misleading scale, exercise and mass.

Atom is not a small solar system.

It's the least and the easiest thing to do wrong to paint a planet around the sun.10⁻¹⁵-10⁻¹⁴ m2 of the atom core diameter and 10⁻¹⁰ m2 of the atom; four to five times of magnitude. If the atom core is amplified into a glass bead, the electron cloud will be as large as a stadium, not a few fine tracks. [1][3] More importantly, Electron has no track to track. Quantum mechanics gives probabilities distribution: how much chance we can find Electron near a certain location, but it cannot be said to be “on a line at the moment”. The term electron cloud is not a vague term, but rather a description of the statistical distribution of the results of measurement. [1][4]

Mass is in the core, volume in the clouds.

The vast majority of atommass is concentrated in nuclear. Proton and neutron weigh about 1836 times more than electron, so an atom mass is almost determined by the inside particles; but the core is only a tiny part of the volume. Mass and volume's “fault”, makes density, dispersion and penetrating experiments look intuitive. [3][5] Volume is not a hard-bundary. The atom radius depends on how far the Electron cloud decays and what definition is used. One definion changes the radius, but it's still near10⁻¹⁰. [2] [4]

The word "empty" is the wrong way to ask questions.

If the word “empty” is understood to mean “no mass, no site, no detectable interaction”, it is clear that there is no space inside atom. There is a nuclear charge, electron has a charge and voltage, and the electromagnetic field is filled around it; particles are colliding, dispersing or stimulating through the substance. [1][3] Accuracy's question is: "What structure inside the atom is detectable with how much energy and momentum? "The low-energy experiments see the overall response of the electron clouds, so the high-energy dispersion can distinguish between the core and the quark. There is a difference between what you see and what you see. [4] [5]

How?

Rutherford scatters gold with alpha particles: mostly through, with a few large angles turning, indicating a concentration of positive charge and mass in small areas. The subsequent electron dispersion, X-ray distillation and spectrometry gradually define the size, level and shape of the nuclear and electron cloud. [1][3] Modern measurement is not based on a “atom photo”. Scan tunnel microscopes, electron microscopes, laser spectrums and accelerator experiments give different scenarios; it's only by putting them together that we have atom images today. [3] [4]

♪ Bang, bang, bang ♪

“99.999% inside atom is empty” is a rough phrase of a volume, not a strict conclusion. It treats the core and the electron cloud as two solid balls, ignoring the field and probability distribution; in the course of chemistry, materials and life, what really works is the overlap and energy of the electron cloud, not the “aerial cavity”. [2][5] Remember three things: the core is small but heavy; the Electron cloud has no orbit; the toom radius is definion. If we separate these three things, the atom will no longer be painted as a little solar system. [1][4]

In-depth reading: scaleconversion exercise

If the atom diameter is considered10⁻¹⁰rice, and the nuclear diameter is 10⁻¹⁵ rice, the nuclear diameter is only one tenth of the atom diameter; at volume, the ratio is also squared about 10⁻¹⁵. This number is often reduced to 99.999%, which is empty, but it only exists when both the nuclear and the Electron cloud are considered solid balls. [1][3]

Read deep: Why chemistry only looks at electron clouds

Chemical reactions involve overlaps, sharing and transfer of Electron clouds, with energy scale usually in eV order of magnitude; nuclear reactions involve MeV order of magnitude, which is one million times different. Therefore, chemistry does not easily change the atom nuclear, and the identity of element remains unchanged in the chemical reaction. [1][4]

Deep reading: Dispersion and Tunnel

Rutherford Scatter has proved to be very small; deep, non-flexible Scatter further sees Quake. The scanning tunnel microscope uses the Electron cloud to pierce currents between the tip of the needle and the sample to distinguish the surface atom, but is still not “seeing” a static ball. [3][5]

Read deep: mass and volume's measrementborn

Atommass can be measured in mass spectrometry, with atom radius based on distillation, spectra and theory; the two are not derived from the same ruler. The "density" that separates mass from volume is the average measration convance, which is not equal to the density of the core or part of the Electron cloud. [2][5]

Continue: Nuclear marbles

If the core is amplified to the size of the marble, the atom diameter can be amplified to hundreds of metres or even kilometres; the number depends on the selection of the atom and the nuclear radius. This metaphor is used only to create a sense of proportionality and does not mean that the elecron cloud has a clear outer wall. [1][3]

Continue: Electron clouds and chemical keys

When two atoms approach, electron clouds overlap first and energy changes with distance; key lengths and keys are given by quantum mechanics and spectrometry. Think of electron as a small ball around the core, which does not explain directional versus co-price keys. [1][4]

Continue: mass gap

The sum of atommas and nuclear proton, neutronmass is not exactly the same, and the difference exists in the form of a combination. The mass gap is extremely small in atoms and must be taken into account in nuclear physics and integration calculations. [3][5]

Continue: Measurement units

Air rice (10⁻¹⁵ m) is commonly used in the nuclear radius, as well as platinum (10⁻¹² m) or e (10⁻¹⁰ m) in the atom radius. The combination of units makes the conclusion of “a few differences of magnitude” look several times different. [2] [4]

Continue: teaching model

The planet model is suitable for “nuclear at the center” and not for “where is Electron”. More accessible classroom models are probability cloud-enabled maps; the division of labour between the two is not a substitute for each other. [1][5]

Something to look back on.

Compress the page into a single sentence: the atom nuclear is small, heavy, electron clouds are large and have no fixed orbit; the "empty" is a volume metaphor, not a physical conclusion. The internal structure of the atom depends on detecting the transfer of energy and momentum: low-energy experiments see the overall response of the electron cloud, so that high-energy dispersion can distinguish between core and quark. Chemical keys, material strength and life processes occur between electron clouds, not in the "aerial cavity". Remember that with a nuclear radius using fly rice, atom radius using picome or e, a combination of units, the scale sense is several times of magnitude. [1][3][5]
