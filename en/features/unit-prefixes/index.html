Unit prefixes: k, M, G and μ, n, p

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The SI word tied 10 to units: k=10³, M=10⁶, G=10⁹, m=10⁻³, μXQPH4QZX, n=10⁻⁹. Area and volume are squared/cubic.

Phrase

SI Grouped by10³: k thousands, M millions, G billions, T trillion; mm, μ, n, p, f They are added to the unit without changing the unit itself. [1][2] Words are not separate units. 1 km is 1,000 m, not "other metre"; conversion of words to a ten-fold term before computing. [1][5]

Pocket of volume area

Word function: 1 km2= (10³m)2=10⁶m2; 1 cm3= (10⁻²m)3=10⁻⁶. Consider 1 km2 as 1000 m2, a thousand times less. [1][5] Volume flows, density and pressure are often accompanied by words. 1 MPa =10⁶Pa, 1 mL = 10⁻⁶; replace all quantities with the same header and replace the formula. [2][5]

Special cases for kg

The kg is the SI base unit and already contains "thousands". 1 mg =10⁻⁶kg, 1t =10³kg; do not write “1 kkg”. Mass's headline added to the g is to pass through kg conversion. [1][4] Time s can be added, but Min, h, d is not SI; 1 ms=10⁻³s, 1 min=60 s cannot be "kmin" as standard. [1][5]

Binary Word

Ki, Mi, Gi is a 2-year-old, while k, M, G is a 10-year-old; 1 KiB = 1024 B, 1 kB = 1,000 B. Storage labels are not consistent with operating system displays and often come from both sets of terms. [5] [6] Network speed is commonly used for 10 digits, memory is commonly used for 2 digits; unit and meaning are harmonized when comparing 1 Gbps and 1 GiB/s. [5] [6]

I'm sorry.

Words can only be used in SI units, not simply in non-SI units such as “persons” “years”; “thousands” can be said, but “k” is not a normative unit. [1][2] No change in words. 1.0 km still has only two signatures valid figures; after 1,000 m, it cannot be written as 1000.0 m implying higher precision. [2] [4]

In-depth reading: Word rule

Words can only be added to the unit and cannot be duplicated with other words; 1 mkm is not standard. In complex units, the header is usually added to the first unit or to the unit as a whole, specifically by SI norms and context. [1][2]

Read deep: common mistakes

Use kg as a normal combination of "thousands times grams" and km2 as 1000 m2, mix KiB with kB and use "kmin" as a standard unit. Each error corresponds to an index or benchmark confusion. [1][5]

In-depth reading: practice

Change 3.2 GW to W, 450 nm to m, 2.5 cm3 to m3, and 1.5 GiB to B; start with the header value, multiplier, final check spectrometry and magnitude. [1][5]

The SI words bind 10 gills to units: k, M, G, T and m, m, n, p each with a fixed index.
