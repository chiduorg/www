Dimensional analysis: units can check your equations

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

The schematic describes the basic properties, such as length, mass, time, in units that are the specifics of the schematic. The equations must be identical; however, the equations are not the correct formula.

It's not a unit.

The schematic is an attribute such as " length " "mass " "time ", the unit being its specific scale. The distance can be expressed in m, km, ly, all the scales are L; time can be used in s, min, yr, all the scales are T. [1][2] The velocity is L/T, the acceleration is L/T2, the force is M L/T2, and the energy is M L2/T2. The matrix is written in square brackets to facilitate examination of the formula. [1][5]

Check Formula

The equations must be identical. v=d/t Left L/T, Right L/T, OK; if v=d t, right becomes L t, the error is immediately disclosed. [1][5] Adding or subtracting requires a symmetric outline; multiplication can be combined. Log, ext and trigonometric functions usually require no schematic input, otherwise the result depends on unit selection. [2] [4]

Export Unit

SI export units are grouped by basic units: N=kg.m/s2, J=N=m=kg.m2/s2, W=J/s. The unit in the formula is in place to see which unit to use. [1][2] In units, the schematics remain unchanged, only the scale changes. 1 L =9.46×10¹⁵m; review should be L, not light-year. [2][5]

No Outline

Reno, Mah, and fine structural constants are all without symmetry and represent a ratio of two equivalents. They have no unit, but they have a physical significance; when changing scale, no scale determines whether flow or behaviour is similar. [2] [4] Quantification analyses can guess the form of formulae, such as single cycles, which can only be combined by L, g; but the first 2 gills need an experiment or theory. [4] [5]

I'm sorry.

Consistency is only a necessary, not a sufficient condition. v=d/t corresponds to v=2d/t schematics, but the coefficient is different; the schematic analysis cannot replace measurement and model. [1][4] Empirical formulas often contain coefficients with schematics, and after-unit coefficients are changed. Don't think of "the correct" as "physically correct." [4] [5]

In-depth reading: units and scales

The same array can be in different units: m, km, ly; time s, min, yr. The schematic check does not look at the specific unit, but the value value depends. The two are separated in order to check both formulas and data. [1][5]

In-depth reading: inspection steps

Each symbol is written into a symmetrical schematic, then into an equation; it is simplified and then compared. If there is no consistency, priority is given to checking whether the formulae are copied, the unit conversion is missing or not; no data are suspected. [1][4]

Read deep:

Quantum analysis precludes errors and does not prove correct. Coefficients, function forms and physical mechanisms still need to be experimented with or theoretically; in particular, an independent measurement is needed for the absence of a scalar constant. [4] [5]

Continue: Quantum symbol

Length L, mass M, time T is a common base scale; speed L/T, strength M L/T2, energy M L2/T2. Write the symbols first, then replace them. [1][5]

Continue: check formula

The equations shall be identical; the additions and subtractions of the requirement equations shall be multiplied by the combination. When the matrix is inconsistent, check the formula and unit conversion. [1][4]

Continue: unit

The matrix is unchanged, variable in units; 1 ly=9.46×10¹⁵m, conversion is L. Don't put light-year whentime. [2][5]

Continue: No scalding

The Renault, the Mach and the fine structural constants have no units but are of physical significance; they determine similarities and currents. [2] [4]

Continue: baseary

Consistency is a necessary, not sufficient, condition; coefficients and functions still need to be tested or theoretically. Do not treat " symmetry" as "formula pairing". [4] [5]

Something to look back on.

The schematic is the basic properties of length, mass, time, etc., and the units are its specifics. The equations must be uniform: speed L/T, strength M.L.T2, energy M.L2/T2. The schematic examination excludes errors, but does not prove the formula to be correct, and coefficients and functions are still subject to experimentation or theory. No schematics (Reno, Mah, fine structural constants) determine similarities and currents. The coefficients of the empirical formula shall be transposed as well as the after-unit coefficients; "Systems" shall not be considered as "physical pairs". [1][2][4][5]
