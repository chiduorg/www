From the Planck length to the observable universe: why the middle is empty

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Between Plank length and Proton there are about 20 order of magnitude, with no complex objects known. It's not that space is really empty, it's that we don't have a direct identification of the scale probe, or a set of quantum gravity images that have been tested.

Two Endpoints

The length of Planck is approximately1.6×10⁻³⁵ metres, made up of gravitational constants, Planck constants and light speeds, usually used as the lower limit for “known physics to talk about lengths”. [2] Proton charge radius is about0.84 femtometres, i.e.8.4×10⁻¹⁶ metres. [3] The difference between the two is about 5×10¹⁹, close to 20 order of magnitude.

If the diameter of 8.8×10²⁶ is included, the length of Planck to unversescale is about 61 order of magnitude. What's really disturbing is not at both ends, but at the middle: from 10⁻³⁵ meters to ZXXQPH2QX meters, we don't have a list of objects that can be filled up like atoms, cells, planets. [1]

Empty doesn't mean nothing.

This scale is not a physical vacuum. Quantum field theory allows for the constant creation and extinction of a void of particles, a vacuum with zero power, and the space and time themselves may be highly volatile on Plank scale. [6] The problem is that these processes have no identifiable form of “objects”: they are not atoms of Small One or black holes of Small One.

Known composite structures stopped near Proton. Proton consists of quarks and glues, but quarks have no experimentally measured limited radius; lightweights are also treated as spot particles. And down, candidate theories such as string, circle quantum, cause and effect set give different space images, but none has been confirmed by experiments. [5] So the phrase “in the middle” is more accuracy:** the known object level is broken here and the unknown theory is not yet on. **

Why can't you just look in the microscope?

To distinguish a length, the detection wavelength must be shorter than it. The shorter the wavelength, the higher the single photo energy. Upon arrival of10⁻¹⁸ metres, the required energy has entered the energy zone of the Large Power Collider; the additional advance to Planck scale requires more than a dozen orders of magnitude over the current accelerator. [1][6]

Universe rays occasionally bring higher energy particles, but their direction, energy and frequency of arrival are not controlled and cannot be duplicated as in a collision machine. Astronomerization is the opposite: the higher the energy tends to correspond to the less photons, the harder it is to come up with a map that can be rebuilt. [6]

The difficulty of quantum gravity.

In Plane, gravity and quantum effects are the same as order of magnitude, and gravity cannot be treated as a fixed background, as it is for Electromagnetic Fields. There is a direct conflict between the principles of smooth space and quantum mechanics in a broad relativity. [5] This is not a technical question of “combining the two formulas”, but rather a conceptual question of whether “time is still the basic parameter” and whether “distance is meaningful”.

Several options give different prognosis: some say that time and space are scattered in Plank scale, others say that there is a minimum length, and others that black holes leave a stable trail when they evaporate to Planck mass. They can all avoid contradictions in one corner, but the current experiment is not separate. [6]

How do you read this page?

When reading this scale, it was easier to be taken away by the phrase “the smallest pixel of the length of Planck is unverse”. It's just a quantitative threshold from a combination of constants, not a grid already detected. [2][4] Similarly, the “proton radius 0.84 femtometre” is not an absolute precision point; a slightly different value has been given in the different measurement methods, suggesting that even the error of this end point needs to be taken seriously. [3]

A more conservative reading is that it is seen as a -resolution map: from atom to universe, each layer has a corresponding observation tool; below the proton, the tool goes from “imaging” to “dispersion and collision”; and to Plankdale, the tool goes from “experiment” to “theorization”. The fact that there is no list of objects in the middle does not mean that there is no physics in the middle, but rather that we have not been able to make that part of physics a repeatable object. [1][5]

A resolution list.

Line known probes by length, and a clear division of labour can be seen. Atom radius of approximately10⁻¹⁰ metres, with X-ray crystallography, electron microscopes and scanning probes; atom core of approximately 10⁻¹⁵ metres, with Rutherford and Electron scattering; an internal proton radius of approximately ZXXQPH2QZX metres, with deep, non-flexible scattering; a weak scale of approximately ZXXQPH3QZX metres, with high-energy impactors. [1][6]

And then down, the energy demand of the probe rises inversely. To increase the resolution by one order of magnitude, it is necessary to increase energy by one order of magnitude; from10⁻¹⁸ meters to ZXXQPH1QZX meters, by 17 order of magnitude, well beyond any planned accelerator. [1]

So this "empty" is not an omission of the mission, but a layer of its capability. Each layer has its own object, its own instrument, its own error language; mixing different layers of conclusions gives rise to the illusion that there is nothing in the UNiverse. [2][5]
