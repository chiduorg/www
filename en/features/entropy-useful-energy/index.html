Entropy and useful energy: why energy can become less useful

Machine-assisted draft · native review pending

Entropy is often translated as “unordered”, but even more accuracy is a measure of microstatus. Second law of othermodynamics does not say that energy will disappear, but rather that it will become more diffused and difficult to function in transformation; the long-term limit of this “exergy” is that.

Entropy is not "dirty."

The common common interpretation of intropy is “irregularity”, but it is not strictly definition. Statistical mechanics wrote an entropy intoS=k_Bln W, of whichW is the microstate of the system available under given macro conditions, and ZXXQPH2QZX is the Boltzmann constant. The larger the entropy, the more microlevels it can reach. [1][3]

“Houses disordered” are often referred to as a metaphor because the micro-states of a disorderly order are usually much more complex than the organized. But entropy is not just about visual order: atoms in crystals can be organized in a structured manner but they still vibrate entropy; gas mixing, heatdiffusion, loss of information can add entropy. [1]

Microstate Number

Imagine a deck of cards, in order of order, in only a few ways, and the corresponding rankings are huge. The system moved spontaneously from a “minority ranking” to a “majority ranking”, not because of a hand pushing, but because the majority ranking occupied the vast majority of possibilities. [1][3]

This probabilistic argument explains why the second law of other modynamics is not an absolute prohibition but a statistical rule. A small number of particles in short times may see a decrease or drop in entropy; the number of particles in macrosystems is very large, and the probability of reverse rise or fall is too low to be seen within the age of unverse. The “necessity” of intropy is statistically necessary. [1]

I don't know what you're talking about.

According to second law of othermodynamics, the sum of isolated systems is not automatically reduced. A more complete presentation involves systems and the environment: a system that assigns entropy to the environment, thereby making itself more orderly, but the sum of the system and the environment increases. Life, refrigerators and city maintain local order by inputting energy and releasing waste heat. [2] [4]

Second law of othermodynamics is not synonymous with "energy persistence". The first law says total energy is constant; second law of othermodynamics says the quality of energy will decline. Energy can be transferred from a high-temperature heat source to a low-temperature environment, but in turn it does not occur spontaneously unless the outside world works. [2]

Heat engine and Kano.

Heat engine converts heat into power and must work between high-temperature heat sources and low-temperature heat sinks. Carnot efficity gives a ceiling:eta=1-T_C/T_H. This is not the details of the project, but the direct result of second law of the othermodynamics: if only a single temperature, heat engine cannot continuously output power. [2]

Real engines are less efficient than the Kano limit because of friction, heat spreads, and incomplete combustion and exhaust losses. Automobile cooling fluids remove waste tropicals to radiators, which is the point where entropy is released to the environment. Without cryogenic heat, the engine cannot work continuously. [2] [4]

I'm sorry, exergy.

"exergy" or "fire" describes what really works in energy. The energy of the same joule is more useful in a high temperature heat source than in a low temperature environment. Energy has not disappeared, but it has moved from concentration, availability to fragmentation and inaccessibility. [2]

That is why the “energy crisis” is not just about total energy. The energy the Earth receives from the sun is roughly balanced with that from radiation into space, but what we can use is the low intropy energy from the sun and the chemical gradient in Fossil fuel. After it's over, the energy is still, just turned into a waste heat that is hard to do. [2] [4]

Life and local order.

Life is an orderly structure, but life does not violate the rules. Humans consume lowentropy food and oxygen, ejecting carbon dioxide, water and waste heat, and transferring it to the environment. An adult maintains his body temperature with metabolism, which is essentially an ongoing exchange. [2] [4]

It's one-sided to think of life as "retropy." More accuracy says that life sustains local lowentropy in the open system while increasing the total environment. There is no contradiction between local order and global growth. [2]

Entropy and Information

Entropy also appeared in the information theory, but entropy was not the same amount as his mother. The message measures the uncertainty of a probability distribution; the microstates of the physical system are measured by the information. The two share mathematical forms and cannot simply consider “document indestruct” or “loss of information” as adding to their mothermodynamicsentropy. [1][3]

Combining the two concepts makes it easy to conclude that “collating information can reverse universentropy”. Information processing can reduce some local uncertainty, but computers, servers and dissipation systems can release intropy to the environment. Information and energy are the sum, not just the order on the screen. [1][4]

Heat death

In closed or accelerated universe, the formation of Stars will stop, Galaxy will fade, the black hole evaporates, and the material decay, temperature is approaching very low. Heat death is not “universe suddenly cold”, but exergy's gradient disappears and no longer works. [4] [5]

The year10¹⁰⁰ is a pointer of magnitude, not a precise moment; different particle physics assumptions change the evaporation of the black hole and the time of the protondecay. It describes the "last what can happen" roundabout rather than a date that can be used. [4]

Common misunderstandings

The first misunderstanding is that “entropy is chaos”. Entropy is a logarithmic of microstates, and chaos is only a visual analogy of some systems. The second misperception is that “second law of othermodynamics prohibits local order”. Local order can be exchanged for environmental intropy, as in the case of life. [1][2]

The third misconception is that “energy disappears when it is exhausted”. Energy is constant, it's exergy that disappears. The fourth misconception is that “heat death has happened”. It is based on the current long-term model assumptions of universe science and particle physics and is not an event of observation. [2] [4]

Reading

See the "entropy increase" and ask first where the system is: isolated, closed or open. Seeing "energy," ask if it's total, free or exergy. When the quality and quality are clear, second law of thermodynamics will not become an empty word. [1][2]

The concept of intropy connects energy, information and order, but it does not provide moral judgement. To say that the world is doomed to chaos is to turn statistical patterns into emotions; to say that life can reverse entropy is to confuse local and global. Both statements go beyond physics. [1][4]
